Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-04-26 — 3 patterns | Clone your top performer's playbook

The most useful pattern surfacing in late April is methodological: Vercel and SaaStr both built outbound sales agents by shadowing their best human for six weeks and documenting every step before anyone wrote a line of agent code. Vercel cut a 10-person SDR team to 1; SaaStr's agents now do the work formerly handled by 10 SDRs and AEs supervised by 1.2 humans. The recipe drops directly into Jenn's IEC practice and Capital Educators — when the operator is the playbook, document the playbook. Three to four weeks of recordings of how Jenn handles the routine 70% (activity-list reviews, school-list narrowing, parent updates) becomes the agent's operating procedure. Agent reads new student threads, drafts replies in her voice as Gmail drafts, she edits and sends. Around $80-150/month. Pitch: clone your voice for the routine 70% so you spend the other 30% on the strategic calls only you can have. Same setup at Capital Educators (diagnostic debriefs, score-plateau parent calls) and The Music Space (trial-lesson follow-ups, recital questions, tuition-bump conversations).

Notion 3.4 shipped Custom Agent Autofill on April 14 — databases that maintain themselves. The trigger is a scheduled cron or row modification; the agent reads the row plus linked Slack/Gmail/Calendar context via MCP, runs a saved multi-step prompt, and writes structured output back into a column. For Jenn, this means moving her Sunday-night Google Sheet update to a Notion database where columns like current_essay_status, next_deadline_30d, and flags_to_review fill themselves overnight against live email and tracker data. Stack is Notion Business plus Custom Agent runtime, around $30/seat plus ~$58/month per agent in runtime once the May 3 free trial ends. Replaces the Sunday spreadsheet update and the "wait, where is Sophie on her Common App?" recall problem on Monday morning calls. Same model fits Capital Educators (last_practice_score, score_trend_4wk) and The Music Space (recital_readiness_flag, parent_engagement_signal_30d). Pitch: every student row tells you the truth Monday morning without you opening Gmail.

The third pattern is the dormant-lead reactivation agent SaaStr is running at 70% response rates on cohorts humans wrote off. Scheduled scan of the CRM for leads aged 60-180 days without contact, agent reads the original thread plus any new context (LinkedIn, public news), drafts a hyper-personalized reactivation referencing the specific thing they cared about and a specific reason to come back now. Sends through Gmail, escalates positive replies to Slack, archives the rest. For Jenn — every fall the spring/summer ghosts get one well-timed Sunday-night touch ("saw Tess is a junior now — we still have August-start slots"). Around $50-80/month on top of HubSpot Free or Pipedrive Lite. For Capital Educators, run it in late July when score panic peaks and the diagnostic-takers-who-didn't-enroll get personalized notes referencing the specific test their kid took. For Sollos, the list is trade show buyers Stephen pitched who didn't convert — trigger when there's a real reason to come back (new flavor, new retail win), agent drafts a note referencing what each buyer specifically cared about in the original conversation. Around $80-120/month. Pitch: every lead that ghosted you gets one well-timed touch while you sleep.
