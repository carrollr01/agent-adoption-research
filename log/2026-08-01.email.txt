Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-08-01 — 5 patterns | Sentence-triggered CRM code-execution agent

https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/determined-meitner-5au7ax/log/2026-08-01.md

Five patterns today. Three are net-new mechanism families for the log, one is a cleaner variant of a family we've touched, and the fifth is a back-office reconciliation shape that translates in every direction. Two-month gap since the last run so the window was generous.

The freshest one is Lightfield, a CRM that shipped a Skills + Knowledge surface in April and had around 3,000 paying SMB customers using it in production by June. A user types a sentence in Slack or in the app — "draft re-engagement emails for every prospect gone cold in the last 60 days" — and the agent writes Python inside a sandbox with access to the CRM object model, runs it, does the bulk work, and writes back to CRM records. It's what you'd normally ping an ops-savvy team member to do. The copies are obvious. On Clio, a partner texts "close and archive every matter inactive for 90+ days and send the wrap-up letter" and the paralegal Friday cleanup happens. On Mindbody, the manager types "message every member who hasn't checked in in 21 days with the current win-back offer" and the retention list that never gets run gets run. The mechanism is portable to anywhere an SMB has a system-of-record with an API and an owner who keeps asking someone else to run a one-off task.

Nory is running a forecast-driven ordering assistant in production across multi-site restaurant groups in the UK and EU. Trigger is the per-supplier order cutoff. The agent reads a demand forecast (POS, weather, event calendars, promos), joins it to recipe bills of materials and current stock, and drafts a PO that hits predicted demand instead of back-filling to a static par level. Manager nudges for local knowledge, approves, PO transmits. Pilot data cites 8-12% waste reduction. The move is copyable directly into vet clinics doing weekly Rx and consumable orders on Patterson or MWI (procedure counts as the analog for recipes), and into independent grocers hitting UNFI or KeHE cutoffs.

Numeo is targeting owner-operator through mid-size trucking carriers with an agent that watches every load board continuously, filters against dispatcher rules, drafts and negotiates broker emails within margin rules, and books freight into the TMS on acceptance. They pulled voice-calling out of the product and shipped email-only, which reads as a real product making live scope decisions. The generalizable shape — poll a public opportunity feed, filter against a persona, draft and negotiate outreach — copies to small construction subs on RFP portals (BidNet, state and municipal feeds) and to independent commercial insurance producers shopping submissions against carrier appetite guides.

The dental industry has multiple named vendors (Overjet, Curve, Zuub) running eligibility verification agents in production across 300+ payers. Night-before cron reads the appointment roster, logs into each payer portal (or hits their API), pulls code-level benefits, and writes a structured benefits summary back into Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, or Curve. Front desk arrives to a schedule with every coverage question already flagged. Same skeleton copies cleanly to optometry (RevolutionEHR + VSP/EyeMed), audiology, and physical therapy (WebPT + payer portal for visits remaining and auth expiration).

Last one is back-office reconciliation. A named auto shop's WickedFile-style agent flagged $4,200 in missed vendor credits its first month and dropped monthly reconciliation from 40+ hours to under 8. Nightly cron or a vendor statement email triggers it; it parses PDFs, joins line items against parts purchases and warranty returns in the shop management system, chases missed credits, and books journal entries into QuickBooks. Copyable directly to restaurants reconciling Sysco and US Foods statements against Toast POS actual usage, and to vet clinics reconciling monthly Patterson or MWI statements against Cornerstone dispensing.

Noted platform change worth carrying into any of the above: Anthropic put Managed Agents (cron schedules + credential vault) into public beta June 10. That's the plumbing that turns most of these from "requires n8n babysitting" into "point Claude at a schedule and a credential vault." Materially changes the maintenance burden a two-person shop takes on when it ships one of these.
