# 2026-04-30 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
The outbound reactivation voice agent — agent queries the CRM/PMS for dormant customers on a schedule, places the call, books or rebooks the appointment, and writes the result back. Best fit on our pipeline is Caped (lapsed students between test cycles) and Jenn (past clients with younger siblings).

## Patterns

### 1. The reactivation agent that calls your dormant list — `caped`, `music-space`, `jenn`
**Source:** [DentalBase / DentiVoice product update](https://www.dentalbase.ai/blogs/product-updates/dentivoice-automated-patient-follow-up), [Viva AI 2026 DSO market map](https://www.getviva.ai/dental-ai-dso-market-map-2026/), [Pete & Gabi reactivation roundup](https://www.petegabi.com/2026/01/06/the-10-best-ai-reactivation-agents-to-bring-lost-customers-back/) — DSO and dental practice voice tools that run outbound recall and reactivation campaigns at scale, multilingual, with claimed 5-7x cheaper economics than acquiring a new patient. Distinct from the inbound booking agent (Avoca, 4/17) and the insurance-claim-status agent (LunaBill, 4/24) — this one originates calls from a CRM segment query, not a ringing line and not a stuck claim.
**Mechanism:** Trigger is a daily scheduled query against the practice management system or CRM — "patients with last_visit > 18 months and recall_date < today" — plus a configurable cap on dial volume. Agent reads the patient record, last appointment notes, insurance status, and any prior outreach attempts, then dials with a script that acknowledges the gap, offers the next-available slot, and routes objections. On a successful book it writes the appointment back to the PMS, tags the patient as reactivated, and stops further outreach. On voicemail it drops a personalized message and triggers a follow-up SMS the next day. Replaces the staffer running batched recall campaigns by hand once a quarter, and the recall reports nobody actions.
**Why agentic:** It's a closed loop — query, decide who to call today, hold the conversation, complete the booking, write back, decide who to retry tomorrow. A dashboard would surface the same dormant list and wait for a human to act on it; this one acts and stops.
**SMB translation:**
- *SAT/ACT prep (Caped, Debbie):* trigger is a weekly query of the CRM for students who finished a previous test cycle and haven't re-enrolled for the next one, plus parents whose older child finished but who have a younger one within 12-24 months of test age. Agent calls the parent, references the prior student's score gain, offers the spring or summer cohort, and books a free consult on Debbie's calendar if interested. Stack is the existing CRM (HubSpot or whatever Caped runs) plus Retell or Vapi for voice plus Claude for the script logic plus Google Calendar for the booking. Around $300-500 a month at Caped's volume. Replaces the manual reactivation calls Debbie's team makes between cycles, badly. Pitch: every previous family gets a real call from your office before each test window without anyone on your team picking up the phone.
- *Music studio (Music Space, Justin):* trigger is a weekly Opus1 query for students who paused or dropped lessons more than 60 days ago. Agent calls the parent, references the instrument and the last teacher, offers a return slot with the same teacher if available, and books it directly into Opus1. Around $200-300 a month. Replaces Justin re-engaging dropped students one Sunday a month and forgetting half of them. Pitch: every dropped student gets a real outreach call within 90 days, you stop losing the families who just got busy.
- *Independent college counselors (Jenn):* trigger is a quarterly query of the client list for families whose oldest finished the application cycle 2-4 years ago and have a younger sibling now in 9th-11th grade. Agent calls the parent, references the older sibling's outcome, and offers an intro consult for the younger student. Stack is the CRM (CollegePlannerPro or her current system) plus Vapi plus Calendly. Around $200 a month. Replaces nothing because most IECs never run sibling-reactivation campaigns at all. Pitch: every family who's already vouched for you hears from you again before they go shopping.

### 2. The aerial-image-to-quote agent for property work — `landscaping`, `pest-control`, `general`
**Source:** [Jobber lawn care cost calculator](https://www.getjobber.com/free-tools/lawn-care-cost-calculator/), [Duranta landscaping AI engine](https://getduranta.com/blog/best-software-for-aerial-measurements), [GreenPal AI tools roundup for lawn care 2026](https://www.yourgreenpal.com/blog/top-ai-tools-for-lawn-care-businesses) — aerial-imagery measurement tools have moved from $200/measurement contractor add-ons to real-time API endpoints that hit 95-99% accuracy on residential lots, and Jobber AI now drafts a quote from past quote templates in seconds. The pieces are sitting next to each other; the agent stitches them.
**Mechanism:** Trigger is a new lead from the website contact form, a Google Local Services Ads call transcript, or an inbound text — anything that contains a property address. Agent geocodes the address, calls a satellite-measurement API to get lawn square footage, bed area, hardscape, and edging linear feet, then runs the numbers through the company's pricing matrix (per sqft mowing, per linear foot edging, per cubic yard mulch). It generates a Jobber quote with itemized lines, drops the aerial screenshot into the quote PDF, and texts the customer a one-tap approve link. Approved quotes auto-schedule into the next available route slot. Writes the lead, the quote, and the schedule entry to Jobber. Replaces the owner driving out for a $50 estimate that takes an hour round-trip plus another 30 minutes building the quote at his desk that night.
**Why agentic:** It chains decisions — geocode, measure, classify property type, apply pricing rules, generate output, route to schedule — without a human in the loop unless the property fails a validation rule (irregular lot, commercial, gated). The traditional flow is a human looking at a map and a price sheet; this one closes the loop from inquiry to scheduled job.
**SMB translation:**
- *Landscaping (cold-outreach pipeline):* trigger is the website "Get a quote" form. Agent runs the address through Go iLawn or Duranta's API ($25-50/mo flat or per-measurement pricing), pulls the company's standard pricing from a Google Sheet, generates a quote in Jobber, and texts the customer within 5 minutes of submission. Stack is Jobber + a satellite measurement API + Twilio + Make or n8n for the orchestration + Claude for property-edge-case triage. Around $250-400 a month all-in. Replaces the owner doing free estimate visits on Saturdays — a 6-truck operation typically does 12-15 of these a week and closes maybe 30%. Pitch: every web lead gets a priced, scheduled quote before they finish their coffee, and you stop spending Saturdays driving to look at lawns you already could have priced from your phone.
- *Pest control yard treatments:* same trigger, same measurement, different pricing matrix (per sqft for yard mosquito or fire ant treatment). Around $200-300 a month. Replaces the office staff calling the customer back to "schedule a free inspection" when they could just send the price. Pitch: yard treatments get quoted instantly with the actual square footage, not a guess off the address.
- *Restaurants doing event catering with delivery:* not a property fit, skip.
