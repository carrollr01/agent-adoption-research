Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-11-13 — 4 patterns | Cancellation-rescheduling agent that fills the chair from the waitlist

Repo: https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/determined-meitner-ymwjha/log/2026-11-13.md

Four concrete patterns today, all event-driven inside the system of record rather than bolted onto email. The one most worth stealing is Planet DDS's cancellation-rescheduling agent, which treats every late cancel as a trigger to work the waitlist and rebook the chair before the gap becomes lost revenue. The same loop fits any appointment-based SMB with a no-show problem.

1. Cancellation-rescheduling agent that works the waitlist the moment a chair opens (dental, gyms, home-health, restaurants, trades)

Planet DDS has been rolling out DentalOS AI Agents inside Denticon and Cloud 9 since the Feb 26, 2026 launch and has now broken scheduling into four distinct roles — a Scheduling Agent that works recall/waitlist/ASAP/unscheduled-treatment lists, a Confirmation Agent for outbound confirms, a Recall Agent for overdue hygiene, and the one that's actually novel: a Cancellation Rescheduling Agent that fires when a patient cancels and rebooks the chair from the waitlist before the gap becomes lost revenue. The trigger is a cancellation event in the PMS, the agent reads the vacated slot's length and provider plus the waitlist filtered by procedure type and insurance, then it calls and texts candidates in priority order and writes the confirmed booking directly back into the schedule. It replaces the front-desk scramble that usually ends with the slot empty because nobody had the ten minutes it takes to make the five phone calls.

The same loop ports cleanly to vet clinics on Cornerstone or Digitail, where a late cancel fires a Zapier/Make listener against the waitlist and a Vapi or Retell voice number calls pet owners for the same species and visit type. Pitch: "A dog with an ear infection calls to cancel at 10 a.m.; by 10:20 the slot is filled with another dog whose owner has been waiting three days." For a Pilates or CrossFit studio on Mindbody, the trigger is a class drop with two hours to go, the agent texts the waitlist, confirms with the first yes, and writes the reservation back into Mindbody — replacing the owner refreshing the app between sets. For a home-health agency on HHAeXchange or AlayaCare, the trigger is an aide calling out sick, the agent reads the patient's care-plan and the backup-aide roster filtered by proximity and certifications, calls candidates, and lands the reassignment back in the scheduling system.

Source: https://www.planetdds.com/dentalos-ai-agents/

2. Email-triggered multimodal freight quoting agent (logistics, small-construction, manufacturing, restaurants, retail)

C.H. Robinson has spent 2026 turning the broker's inbox into an agent interface. Its generative AI reads inbound email quote requests, extracts load type and route, checks live capacity and pricing, and replies with a quote in an average 2 minutes 13 seconds — about 2,000 a day on the truckload side, and after LTL was added the company reports a 30% month-over-month jump in AI-delivered LTL quotes. In October the pattern was extended into ocean shipping, air freight, and customs brokerage, where a quote used to take days. Over 3 million shipping tasks have now run through these agents. What they replace isn't the broker's judgment — it's the twenty minutes of lookup between every "please quote" email and every reply.

The transferable mechanism is "messy inbound free-text request → structured extraction → live pricing/availability check → quote back in the same thread," and it has room to run outside freight. For a small construction company getting daily RFQs from GCs, the trigger is a new inbound email classified as a materials request; the agent extracts quantity, delivery window, and job site, pings the supplier API or price book, and replies with a quote plus a draft estimate attached, writing a shadow record into HubSpot or BuilderTREND. For a specialty manufacturer taking quote requests through sales@, the agent reads the bill of materials, prices against the ERP, writes a quote PDF, and either sends it for repeat customers or routes for approval for new ones. For a wholesale restaurant supplier, the same loop turns "need 20 cases of ribeye for Saturday" into a priced confirmation in the same channel. Pitch: "Every quote email gets a priced response in two minutes instead of two hours, and your team only touches the ones worth negotiating."

Source: https://www.chrobinson.com/en-gb/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2026/lean-ai-freight-forwarding-agents/

3. Multi-agent claims adjudication with a dedicated audit sub-agent (insurance, small-construction, home-health, accountants, professional-services)

Allianz Australia's Project Nemo, running on food spoilage claims under about AUD$500, routes each claim through seven specialized agents — a planner that orchestrates, a coverage agent that checks policy wording, a weather agent that correlates the outage window with meteorological data, a fraud agent, a cyber agent, a payout agent, and the one that makes the pattern interesting: an audit agent whose only job is to synthesize every other agent's decision into a human-readable rationale and hand it to a person for the actual payment. 80% processing-time reduction, under five minutes from claim to human-ready packet, and the audit agent is what makes human sign-off fast.

The borrowable idea is the audit role, not the insurance case. Most multi-agent workflows ask the human to review opaque output and trust it; this one lets specialists run and then forces a narrator agent to show its work before anyone signs. For a construction change-order loop — contract agent reads the signed scope, site agent pulls progress photos and timesheet notes, cost agent reprices, audit agent writes up what changed and why and sends it to the PM in Procore. For a home-health billing cycle, visit-completion, eligibility, and coding agents each do their piece and the audit agent assembles a one-page chart note the biller signs before Waystar submission. For a CPA firm doing R&D credit studies, dozens of agents pull project data, interviews, and expense ledgers; the audit agent writes the IRS-shaped narrative the partner signs. Pitch: "You don't review ten agents' chat logs — you read one page of reasoning and approve it."

Source: https://coverager.com/allianz-introduces-agentic-ai-to-speed-up-food-spoilage-claims/

4. Agentic workflows compiled from a firm's own SOP (small-law-firms, accountants, dental-and-vet-clinics, insurance, real-estate)

At ClioCon in October, Clio shipped a feature that lets a firm describe a process in plain language or upload an existing SOP, and Clio Work compiles it into an agentic workflow — a sequence of steps the system runs itself, with the lawyer placing approval points at the junctures they want to see. The lead example is matter intake plus conflict check plus engagement letter draft, but Vincent's agentic mode is now in use for roughly 90% of all Vincent work, and it will pull live records from a billion-record federal/state/bankruptcy docket when the workflow calls for it. What this replaces is the gap between "we have an SOP in a Google Doc" and "the SOP actually runs" — the agent is the executor, the lawyer puts the stop signs.

The pattern — operator hands the system their own written process, the system compiles it into a running workflow, and the operator decides which steps need human sign-off — is sitting unused in most verticals that have SOPs in Word or SharePoint. For a dental practice, an owner uploads the "new patient intake" SOP and gets an agent that collects demographics, verifies insurance through a clearinghouse API, prefills forms into Dentrix, and pauses for the front desk to confirm before posting to the schedule. For an accounting firm, the 1040 intake SOP becomes an agent that ingests the engagement letter, chases source docs from the client portal, drops them in the right folders in Karbon, and pauses at the data-entry-to-draft handoff. For a small insurance brokerage, the renewal SOP turns into an agent that pulls the expiring policy from the AMS, requests updated carrier quotes via email, builds the comparison, and pauses before anything goes to the client. Pitch: "The SOP in your shared drive stops being a document people pretend to read and starts being the software."
