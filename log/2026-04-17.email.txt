Daily agent pattern scan for 2026-04-17. Five patterns today. Repo copy: https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/epic-fermat-bfUgi/log/2026-04-17.md

TL;DR: Stripe's "minions" ship 1,300 PRs/week triggered by a Slack emoji reaction. The trigger, not the agent, is the interesting part. A Gmail label as a one-click work order is the shortest path to Jenn's parent-update problem without building her a portal.

1. Reaction-as-trigger task handoff (tag: jenn)
Source: How I AI with Steve Kaliski, 2026-03-25. https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-stripe-built-minionsai-coding
Mechanism: Slack emoji reaction on a message is the trigger. Agent spins up a dev env, reads thread context, opens a PR. No form, no ticket.
Why not a dashboard: the trigger is an ambient human signal inside the tool they're already using.
For Jenn: Gmail label "needs-update" on a student thread. Agent reads the thread, her CPP/CounselMore record, and the last parent update sent. Drafts the next update in her voice as a Gmail draft and pings her "draft ready for Emma Chen." Stack: Gmail API, Claude API, Apps Script label watcher, thin MCP over her CRM export. Replaces ~15 hrs/mo of her Sunday writing at 20 clients. Pitch: "Label the email. Get back a draft. No portal."

2. KPI narrative agent (tag: caped)
Source: LogRocket/Cotera. https://blog.logrocket.com/product-management/ai-powered-slack-workflows-for-product-managers and https://cotera.co/articles/slack-workflow-builder-advanced-guide
Mechanism: Friday 4 PM cron reads a KPI sheet and the prior week's post, computes WoW deltas, writes a summary with causal framing ("volume up 23%, concentrated in Salesforce connector issues"), posts to Slack.
Why not a dashboard: it names the driver and compares against its own prior post. Synthesis, not a chart.
For Caped: Monday 7 AM cron. Reads Blueprint/TestInnovators scores, attendance, tutor notes. Posts one message per cohort to Debbie's Slack or WhatsApp. Stack: Sheets, Claude API, Make.com, about $30/mo. Replaces the director pulling numbers for a Monday email. Pitch: "Skip the dashboard nobody opens. Get the conclusion as a Slack message."

3. Standup collector with follow-up enforcement (tag: music-space)
Source: Cotera. https://cotera.co/articles/slack-workflow-builder-advanced-guide
Mechanism: Scheduled prompt. Tracks who replied. DMs non-responders at +45 min. Counts skips across the week. DMs the manager on the third skip with the specific pattern.
Why not a dashboard: stateful. It remembers, escalates, and takes different actions based on the count.
For Justin at The Music Space: Sunday 6 PM, agent SMSes each teacher a pre-filled list of their 6 students and asks for notes. Nudges Monday 9 AM. Pings Justin on the laggards Wednesday. Teacher notes auto-file into Opus1 for parents. Stack: Opus1 CSV via Make, Twilio, Claude API. Replaces Justin's weekly nagging. Pitch: "Teachers get one SMS. Parents get notes. You stop being the nag."

4. Inbound voice booking for trades (tags: hvac, landscaping, pest-control)
Source: Owned and Operated #173 (Avoca). https://www.ownedandoperated.com/post/owned-and-operated-173-we-booked-400-calls-a-week-how-avoca-ai-is-shaping-home-services
Mechanism: SIP forward on ring-no-answer/after-hours to a voice agent. Agent reads call history, tech calendar, service-area and pricing guardrails. Qualifies, books, confirms via SMS, writes the job to the CRM.
Why not a dashboard: multi-turn conversation that consults live systems and can decline.
For cold outreach: Vapi or Retell plus Claude plus ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro/Jobber. $200-600/mo at SMB volume. Replaces the missed-call voicemail that kills 40% of leads. Pitch: "You already pay for the leads. Stop losing the ones that call after 5 PM." This is the opener for an HVAC or landscaping cold call — ROI math is on the monthly phone bill.

5. Supply-chain ping agent (tag: sollos)
Source: Crisp, Feb 2026. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260211212237/en/Crisp-Launches-AI-Agents-to-Drive-Shareholder-Value-for-CPG-Brands-and-Retailers
Mechanism: Agent monitors distributor/retailer feeds, detects low inventory or a velocity spike, drafts a concrete replenishment rec, contacts the retail buyer directly.
Why not a dashboard: it reaches out. The outreach is the work.
For Stephen at Sollos: the enterprise telemetry doesn't exist at his scale, so flip the direction. Daily cron plus a Gmail watcher on his co-packer and yerba supplier threads. Reads Shopify/QuickBooks volume and the co-packer schedule sheet. If forecast exceeds on-hand or a supplier email contains "pushed/backordered/ETA," drafts an SMS to Stephen with the SKU, the gap, and what to do next. Stack: Gmail, Sheets, Claude, Twilio. About $40/mo. Replaces the 2 AM "do we have enough tea for Whole Foods" spiral. Not an ERP. A pager that wakes up before you do, and only for things that actually matter.
