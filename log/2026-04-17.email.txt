TL;DR: Highest-leverage port today is Avoca's inbound-voice-booking pattern (proven in HVAC/plumbing at 400+ bookings a week) copied into independent dental, veterinary, and small medical practices. Near-identical operational surface — phone-heavy, calendar-constrained, expensive front desk — with near-zero agent penetration and roughly 200k practices in the US. Same mechanism, different PMS.

1. Reaction-as-trigger task handoff (source: software | ports: healthcare, professional-services)
Source: How I AI with Steve Kaliski, 2026-03-25. https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-stripe-built-minionsai-coding
Mechanism: a Slack emoji reaction on a message is the trigger. Agent spins up a dev env, reads the thread for context, opens a PR. No form, no ticket, no branch-cutting. Stripe ships ~1,300 PRs/week this way.
Why not a dashboard: the trigger is an ambient human signal inside the tool you're already using.
Port to healthcare: a Gmail or portal label ("draft-reply," "refill-review") on a patient thread. Agent reads the thread, the chart note in Dentrix/Jane/Kareo, and the last message sent. Drafts a reply in the provider's voice. Replaces 90 min/day of front-desk triage. $200–400/mo. Pitch: label the message, get a chart-aware draft.
Port to professional-services: a label on a client email at a small CPA firm. Agent reads the thread and pulls prior-year return from Karbon or Canopy. Drafts a reply citing the specific notice or line item. $150/mo at a two-partner firm. Pitch: every client email becomes a labeled work order, grounded in their file.

2. KPI narrative agent — anti-dashboard Monday report (source: software | ports: hospitality, fitness)
Source: LogRocket and Cotera. https://blog.logrocket.com/product-management/ai-powered-slack-workflows-for-product-managers and https://cotera.co/articles/slack-workflow-builder-advanced-guide
Mechanism: scheduled trigger reads a KPI sheet and the prior week's post, computes deltas, writes a summary with causal framing, posts to Slack.
Why not a dashboard: it names the driver and compares against its own prior post. Synthesis, not a chart.
Port to hospitality: Monday 7 AM cron reads Toast or Square POS plus 7shifts labor. Owner gets one text per location — "labor 34% vs 28% target, bar overstaffed Fri/Sat; Tuesday covers down four weeks running." $40–60/mo. Replaces the Monday recap nobody reads.
Port to fitness: Monday 6 AM cron reads Mindbody or Mariana Tek. Owner gets one text — "class fill flat at 62%, noon classes dropped fifth week, Jenna's 7 AM up 80." $30/mo. Replaces an hour of logging into Mindbody.

3. Standup collector with follow-up enforcement (source: software | ports: trades, healthcare)
Source: Cotera. https://cotera.co/articles/slack-workflow-builder-advanced-guide
Mechanism: agent prompts each person on a schedule, tracks replies, DMs non-responders at +45 min, counts skips across the week, escalates to the manager on the third skip with the specific pattern. Stateful.
Why not a dashboard: it remembers and takes different actions based on the count.
Port to trades: Friday 4 PM, agent SMSes each tech with his week's completed jobs pre-filled from ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro. Reply captures notes per job. Auto-files into the job record so photos and notes are one tap from the invoice. Nudges Saturday morning, pings ops manager Sunday night on the laggards. $80/mo. Replaces ops-manager nagging on a Sunday.
Port to in-home healthcare: end-of-shift SMS to each visiting nurse with her day's visits pre-filled. Reply captures visit notes per patient, files into WellSky or MatrixCare. Compliance up, retention up because charting finishes before she gets to the car. $100/mo.

4. Inbound voice booking (source: trades | ports: healthcare, hospitality)
Source: Owned and Operated #173 (Avoca). https://www.ownedandoperated.com/post/owned-and-operated-173-we-booked-400-calls-a-week-how-avoca-ai-is-shaping-home-services
Mechanism: SIP forward on ring-no-answer and after-hours to a voice agent. Agent reads caller history, provider calendar, and guardrails. Qualifies, books, confirms by SMS, writes the job to the CRM, declines when rules require.
Why not a dashboard: multi-turn live conversation that queries systems and decides.
Port to healthcare: SIP forward at a dental, vet, or small medical practice. Agent reads patient history in Dentrix, Eaglesoft, ezyVet, or Kareo, provider calendar, and insurance rules. Books cleanings and re-exams, routes emergencies to on-call, files refill requests as tasks. Writes back to the PMS. $300–700/mo. Replaces the front-desk person covering evenings. Pitch: you already pay for new-patient acquisition; stop losing the 38% who call after 5.
Port to hospitality: SIP forward at an independent restaurant. Agent reads Resy or OpenTable availability and house rules, books the table, offers alternates when full. $200–500/mo. Replaces 15 reservations a week lost to an unanswered landline.

5. Supply-chain ping agent (source: cpg-enterprise | ports: hospitality, construction)
Source: Crisp. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260211212237/en/Crisp-Launches-AI-Agents-to-Drive-Shareholder-Value-for-CPG-Brands-and-Retailers
Mechanism: agent watches distributor/retailer feeds, detects low inventory or velocity spikes, drafts a replenishment rec, contacts the buyer directly. The outreach is the work.
Port to hospitality — flip the direction since an independent restaurant has no distributor telemetry: daily cron plus Gmail or SMS watcher on Sysco, US Foods, and specialty-supplier threads. Reads Toast velocity on spoilables and the on-hand sheet. If forecast exceeds on-hand or a supplier email says "pushed" or "OOS," drafts a text to the owner with the SKU, the gap, and the move — call supplier B, sub this cut, 86 the special. $40/mo. Replaces the Sunday inventory spiral and the Wednesday 86-list scramble.
Port to construction: Gmail watcher on lumber yard and supply-house threads. Reads Buildertrend or Procore project schedules and PO statuses. When a delivery slides past the scheduled install, drafts a message to the PM with project, affected trade, and crew to reschedule. $80/mo. Replaces the PM finding out the framing package is three days late when the framers show up.
