Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-05-30 — 5 patterns | medspa ambient scribe is the strongest copy

https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/epic-fermat-fBkmF/log/2026-05-30.md

Five patterns landed this run with mechanism-level detail, hitting sectors we haven't yet covered well: trades, healthcare aesthetics, accounting, owner-operator services with contracts, and cohort-based online education. The highest-leverage copy is the medspa ambient scribe — ambient charting has hardened in primary care, the aesthetics market has the same data shape, and no entrenched vendor owns it yet.

Robby (YC W26) fires off a ServiceTitan job completion. The agent reads the tech's field notes, the customer's equipment age, and third-party property data, then drafts a per-customer next-visit upsell script for the tech and creates a follow-up lead in the GM's queue. Replaces the sales manager that independent HVAC and plumbing shops never staffed. Translates straight into auto repair on Tekmetric or Shopmonkey, and into vet clinics on Cornerstone for vaccine and dental follow-up.

Tepali (YC W26) puts an ambient scribe in the medspa treatment room. The trigger is the session opening in the PMS or a Bluetooth lavalier going active. The agent listens, reads the chart and booked treatment, drafts the SOAP note, updates the injection map with vial lot numbers, and pushes back to the chart for sign-off. The same loop ports straight into dentistry — chair-mounted mic, Dentrix Smart Doc API, draft note plus an inventory decrement on the implant or composite kit. Pitch: your hygienist writes zero notes after lunch.

Byron launched this week with an agent that lives inside the Excel workbook CPAs already use to prep business returns. Import the prior-year 1065 or 1120 plus the client's workpapers, the agent rolls everything forward, drafts the current-year workpaper, and emails the client one consolidated missing-items request keyed to gaps it found. Replaces the staff accountant's 6-12 hour first pass per return. Same loop fits small bookkeeping firms triggered by a Karbon job, reading QuickBooks plus Hubdoc receipts.

Claude for Small Business has a DocuSign contract-review workflow buried in its launch that the announcement skipped over. New envelope arrives, agent compares the inbound PDF against the firm's prior executed agreements, flags non-standard payment terms, liability shifts, and auto-renewal clauses, builds a redline summary in chat. Pitch for owner-operators: every MSA comes back with a summary of what changed from the last one you signed, before you've finished your coffee.

An n8n case study from an 800-student EdTech operator with a 3-person ops team fans out enrollment off a Razorpay payment webhook. The agent creates the LMS account, sends the cohort handbook, adds the student to the WhatsApp Business cohort group, and creates the CRM entry in under 45 seconds. US adaptation: Stripe webhook into Circle, Teachable, Slack Connect, HubSpot. For a Maven-style cohort with 200 enrollments per launch, this turns a two-day enrollment week into a non-event. Same shape works for boutique fitness studios on Mindbody.
