## Patterns

### 1. Voice-Enabled Phone Number Agents
*(tags: `dental-and-vet-clinics`, `home-health`, `restaurants`, `trades`, `small-construction`)*

[PollyReach](https://www.producthunt.com/products/pollyreach) launched on Product Hunt giving AI agents real phone numbers for inbound and outbound calls, with capabilities for bookings, support, and customer follow-up. The trigger is either incoming calls to the assigned number or scheduled outbound calling campaigns based on CRM data or calendar events. Agents can handle appointment scheduling, service inquiries, estimate follow-ups, and payment reminders while maintaining conversation context across multiple calls with the same customer.

For dental practices, agents could receive appointment cancellation calls and immediately offer rebooking options using practice management system integration, then follow up via text with confirmation details. When triggered by no-show appointments in Dentrix or Eaglesoft, the agent could call within 30 minutes offering same-day slots to a waiting list. Using Google Workspace for scheduling, Twilio for calls, and practice management APIs for patient data, this replaces front desk staff making dozens of daily rescheduling calls. The pitch: "Your AI receptionist handles all the appointment juggling while your staff focuses on patient care." For home health agencies, the same pattern works when triggered by aide schedule changes or patient care plan updates, with agents calling families to coordinate visits and calling backup aides to fill coverage gaps. For restaurants, agents could call reservation confirmations, handle table availability inquiries, and follow up on large party bookings, triggered by OpenTable events or manual reservation entries.
