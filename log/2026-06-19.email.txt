Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-06-19 — 3 patterns | Multimodal order intake, pre-meeting briefing, outbound marketplace negotiation

Repo: https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/determined-meitner-c42oal/log/2026-06-19.md

Three concrete patterns surfaced today, each representing a distinct action class. The standout is outbound marketplace negotiation — agents that go out, contact third parties, and execute deals at SMB scale are still rare in the wild.

1. Multimodal order-intake agent for B2B distributors. Choco's OrderAgent and VoiceAgent (OpenAI customer story) process 8.8M+ orders a year across food distributors with reported 95% accuracy and roughly half the manual processing time. Trigger: a restaurant sends an order in any channel — email, SMS, attached spreadsheet, photo of a handwritten note, voicemail, live call. The agent extracts SKUs, quantities, delivery dates and the customer ID, normalizes them against the distributor's catalog, writes a structured order directly into the ERP, and on live calls upsells substitutions. It replaces the night-shift order taker. Generalizable core: inbound business message in any channel, agent normalizes to internal schema, commits to system-of-record, escalates only edge cases. Stack: OpenAI Realtime API plus vertical SaaS plus Twilio plus a queue for asynchronous channels. Direct copies: building-materials wholesalers fielding fax-PDFs and contractor texts, medical supply distributors, auto-parts counters. Scale caveat — needs a clean SKU backbone, or the normalization work eats the savings.

2. Pre-meeting briefing agent triggered by CRM events. Rippling's sales opportunity agent, built by a single Sales Consultant with no engineering, ships in OpenAI's Workspace Agents launch and reportedly saves reps 5–6 hours per week. Trigger: new Salesforce opportunity (or a 6am cron). Reads Salesforce, scrapes recent news, pulls the latest Gong transcript, fetches prior deal notes. Composes a structured deal brief — account context, last call summary, talking points, open objections — and posts it to the rep's Slack channel before standup. Generalizable core: state change in a system-of-record, agent assembles cross-system context, drafts a structured briefing, posts to the channel before the human needs it. Direct copies: a clinic owner gets a morning patient brief before each appointment (EHR plus last call plus outstanding labs); a property manager gets a pre-walkthrough brief on the unit and tenant; a litigator gets a pre-deposition brief on opposing witnesses. Rides on whatever CRM/PMS/EHR the operator already runs. Stack: Workspace Agents or Claude with MCP connectors, alternately n8n with the same connectors. Weekend build for one user.

3. Outbound marketplace negotiation agent for small carriers. Numeo (trucking dispatch) reports Four Ways Cargo, a 150-truck fleet, added ~$1,000/truck/month after adoption, and packages a $99/month tier for carriers running up to 10 trucks. Trigger: a truck nearing drop-off, or a scheduled "find next load" event. The agent scans DAT and other load boards, ranks loads by profit per mile against the truck's home base and historical lane rates, drafts a market-backed counter-offer email to the broker or places an outbound call to negotiate live, and reports results back to the dispatcher in the TMS. Replaces the dispatcher hours spent calling brokers and negotiating rates. This is the novel piece — the agent acts as a counterparty going outbound to third parties, not as an intake or briefing tool. Generalizable core: internal asset becomes available, agent scans external marketplaces, ranks by margin using historical performance, drafts or delivers personalized outreach with a negotiated offer, human approves. Direct copies: hotel revenue managers when rooms open up, freelance contractors auto-bidding on Upwork/RFP feeds with margin-backed quotes, supply houses auto-quoting against material price feeds, dental practices auto-filling no-show slots by texting a ranked waitlist. Less suited to relationship-heavy sales.
