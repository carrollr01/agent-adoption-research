# 2026-06-10 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
The standout today is Lassie's insurance reimbursement reconciliation agent for dental practices: it logs into insurer portals, posts payments into the EHR, verifies the bank deposit, and only kicks exceptions to a human. Two other concrete patterns surfaced — Garvan Institute's genetic variant interpretation drafting agent and Mina's in-call execution agent that mutates CRM, tickets, and calendars mid-meeting rather than after.

## Patterns

### 1. Insurance reimbursement reconciliation agent
**Source:** [Lassie raises $35M Series A — Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/lassie-raises-35m-series-a-to-build-ai-agents-that-handle-medical-practice-administration/) and [Lassie company page](https://www.lassie.ai/) — 700+ U.S. dental practices across 49 states, June 3, 2026 funding announcement.
**Mechanism:** New insurer remittance hits a payer portal or EFT lands in the bank → agent logs into the insurance portal, ingests the remittance, matches each line to the correct insurer/patient/claim in the practice management system (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental), applies the practice's contractual adjustment rules, posts the payment into the PMS, and confirms the deposit through Plaid/MX → exceptions (underpayments, mismatched EFTs, ambiguous write-offs) are flagged to a staff queue rather than left in a backlog → replaces the billing manager's portal-by-portal login loop, line-by-line posting, and end-of-month reconciliation hunt.
**Why agentic:** It navigates third-party portals it doesn't own, applies practice-specific business logic to each line, writes into a Windows-only on-prem system through a local agent bridge, and decides on its own which payments need human review. None of that is a passive display.
**Generalizable core:** External payer event lands in a portal or bank feed → agent fetches it, reconciles it against an internal system of record using business-specific rules, writes the resolved record back, and routes only the unresolved minority to a human.
**Deployability:** Stack is portal scraping + RPA-on-Windows + payer integrations (BCBS, UHC, Delta, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, Guardian) + Plaid/MX + the three dominant dental EHRs. Month-plus build for a vertical-specific clone; the moat is the integration matrix, not the model. Replaces a billing coordinator or shrinks an outsourced billing service. Lassie reports payments hitting accounts in under a week vs. four to five. Works at single-location SMB scale because the local agent is just a Windows installer.

### 2. Genetic variant interpretation drafting agent
**Source:** [Anthropic AI support to accelerate genomics research at Garvan](https://www.garvan.org.au/news-resources/news/anthropic-ai-support-to-accelerate-genomics-research-at-garvan) — Garvan Institute of Medical Research, partnership announced as part of Anthropic's Australian government MOU (AUD $3M Claude credits across four institutions), customer story published June 5, 2026.
**Mechanism:** New genetic variant flagged for clinical classification → agent pulls the relevant slice of population databases, internal lab evidence, and published literature → drafts a structured classification (pathogenic / likely pathogenic / VUS / etc.) with cited evidence per ACMG-style criteria → writes the draft into the variant curation system for a clinical geneticist to accept, edit, or reject → replaces the curator's hours-per-variant literature sweep and evidence assembly, leaving them with the judgment call.
**Why agentic:** It's multi-source synthesis with a structured output and a downstream write, not a chat retrieval. The human is the approver of an agent-produced draft, not the originator.
**Generalizable core:** New record needs an expert judgment → agent assembles all relevant evidence from internal and external sources, drafts the structured determination with citations, and queues it for human approval rather than producing it.
**Deployability:** Stack is Claude API + a corpus loader (PubMed, internal lab DBs, gnomAD-style references) + a structured-output schema + write into whatever curation tool the org uses. Week-to-month build depending on the rigor of the citation/audit trail required. Replaces or augments the early-stage research/curation pass. Caveat: regulated domains (clinical, legal, insurance underwriting) require the draft to be inspectable and re-runnable, which constrains the prompt and forces an explicit evidence-binding step.

### 3. In-call execution agent
**Source:** [Mina Meeting Assistant on Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/mina-meeting-assistant) and [getmina.ai](https://getmina.ai/) — Mina 1.0 launched on Product Hunt; integrates with 200+ tools including HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, Linear, GitHub, Notion, Slack, and the three main meeting platforms.
**Mechanism:** Meeting starts on Zoom / Google Meet / Teams → agent joins, transcribes, and is pre-wired to the team's CRM, ticket tracker, and docs → during the call it identifies commitments and next steps ("I'll send you the proposal Thursday," "open a bug for the auth issue") → before the call ends it files the Jira ticket, updates the HubSpot deal stage, sends the follow-up email draft, and books the next meeting on the calendar → replaces the rep's or PM's post-meeting CRM cleanup and ticket-filing pass.
**Why agentic:** The novelty is the timing: writes to third-party systems while the meeting is still live, not in a batch after. That collapses the "did anyone update the CRM?" question to a yes-by-default.
**Generalizable core:** Live conversation as the trigger → agent extracts commitments in-flight, executes the resulting writes against connected systems before the conversation ends, and confirms to participants while context is still fresh.
**Deployability:** Stack is a meeting-bot SDK (Recall.ai or Vapi-style) + Claude API + your CRM/ticket integrations + a Slack/email confirmation channel. Week-ish build for a single-team vertical (e.g., a managed-services firm with HubSpot + ConnectWise). Replaces or shrinks the AE's "Friday CRM hygiene block" and the PM's post-standup ticket triage. Caveat: in-call writes need explicit guardrails — confirm-before-send for outbound emails and stage changes, otherwise reps will turn it off after one bad commit.
