## Patterns

### 1. The chargeback recovery agent that fights disputes without you — `sollos`, `restaurants`, `general`
**Source:** ChargePay on the Shopify App Store (https://apps.shopify.com/chargepay) and Chargeflow (https://apps.shopify.com/chargeflow) — both are running fully-autonomous dispute response for Shopify merchants in 2026 with reported 85–90% win rates on chargebacks they take on.
**Mechanism:** Stripe/PayPal/Shopify Payments webhook fires when a dispute is opened. The agent reads the order from Shopify (line items, ship address, customer history, IP, device fingerprint, prior support tickets), pulls tracking and delivery confirmation from the carrier, pulls any 3PL or Klaviyo touch logs, and assembles a structured evidence packet (compelling evidence per Visa/MC rules: AVS match, delivery proof, prior undisputed orders, customer login history). It then submits the response via the processor's dispute API before the deadline. It writes the outcome back to a tracking sheet and only escalates to a human for ambiguous "I didn't recognize this charge" friendly-fraud cases above a configurable order value. Replaces a part-time bookkeeper or founder spending 30–60 minutes per dispute pulling screenshots and writing narratives.
**Why agentic:** It's a closed-loop autonomous filing system on a hard external deadline. Reads from 3+ sources, makes a judgment about evidence sufficiency, takes a write action against an external API, and only escalates exceptions. A dashboard would just show you the dispute and let you fight it manually.
**SMB translation:** Stephen at Sollos. Yerba mate is a low-AOV impulse buy and CPG chargebacks are typically friendly-fraud ("I didn't authorize this," "didn't receive it") on $30–$80 orders that are uneconomical to fight manually but losing them at scale wrecks the processor risk score. Trigger: Stripe/Shopify Payments dispute webhook. Stack: n8n or Make on Render, Shopify Admin API + Stripe Disputes API + ShipStation API, Claude API for the narrative draft, Google Drive for evidence archive. Role replaced: the founder spending 45 minutes per dispute or the $25/dispute outsourced VA service. Pitch sentence: "Every chargeback that comes in from Stripe gets a full evidence packet filed within 24 hours without you touching it — and you keep the 85% you'd otherwise lose by not fighting them."

### 2. The property-takeoff agent that prices a job from an address — `landscaping`, `general`
**Source:** SiteRecon AI Agent for landscape and snow estimating (https://order.siterecon.ai/blog/ai-agents-landscape-snow-estimating-guide) — 500+ contractors deployed, 95–98% measurement accuracy, replaces site-visit measuring with conversational input. Also LandscapioAI (https://www.landscapioai.com/) for residential photo-to-quote.
**Mechanism:** Estimator (or an inbound lead form) sends "measure 456 Corporate Drive for snow removal" via SMS, Slack, or a form. The agent geocodes the address, pulls aerial imagery, runs computer vision over it to detect and polygonize parking lots, sidewalks, building perimeters, turf, and beds. It returns square-footage breakdowns by surface type, applies a configured price-per-sq-ft table (with seasonal multipliers and salt/mulch material costs), and produces a branded PDF proposal in 10–15 minutes. Writes the proposal to the CRM as a draft for owner approval and texts the customer a follow-up confirmation. Replaces the 45-minute drive-out, measuring wheel, and back-at-the-office bid assembly — and turns same-week response times into same-hour.
**Why agentic:** It takes a natural-language input, autonomously decides what surfaces to measure based on the service type, runs a vision model, applies pricing logic, generates a document, and writes to a CRM. A dashboard would just show you a satellite image and make you trace it yourself.
**SMB translation:** Cold-outreach landscaping vertical (our queued list). Trigger: inbound web form ("get a quote"), missed call, or estimator text to a shared number. Stack: Twilio for SMS intake, Google Maps Static + a vision model (Claude with vision, or Roboflow for trained polygon detection), n8n for the orchestration, Google Docs API or PandaDoc for the proposal output, HubSpot or whatever CRM the contractor has. For solo operators with no CRM: just write the quote PDF to a shared Drive folder and email it. Role replaced: the estimator's first site visit, which gates everything else. Pitch sentence: "When a homeowner submits your quote form, they get a real PDF estimate with measurements in 15 minutes — before your competitor has even called them back."

### 3. The smart-trap monitoring agent that decides when to dispatch — `pest-control`
**Source:** Trapview's pheromone-trap computer vision system (https://entomologytoday.org/2026/04/08/artificial-intelligence-automate-cockroach-surveillance-pest-management-cities/) and Bright AI for pest control (https://bright.ai/industries/pest-control/) — already running in commercial pest control with real-time monitoring tied to dispatch.
**Mechanism:** A connected trap (camera + sensor in a bait station, sticky trap, or insect light trap) photographs activity on a fixed cadence or motion trigger. The agent runs species ID + count classification on each image, compares against a per-account threshold (e.g., "more than 3 cockroaches in 24 hours at this restaurant location triggers urgent dispatch"), pulls the customer's service contract from FieldRoutes/PestPac, and decides one of three actions: (a) log the data and continue monitoring, (b) schedule the next routine visit with this account flagged as elevated, or (c) auto-dispatch a technician and notify the account manager via SMS. It writes the inspection log back to the CRM so the technician arrives with context. Replaces monthly drive-by inspections that only catch problems after they've gotten bad.
**Why agentic:** It's a perception + reasoning + action loop on a real-world sensor stream. Threshold logic plus contract context plus dispatch decision, end-to-end. A dashboard would just show you photos.
**SMB translation:** Cold-outreach pest control vertical. Most 5–20 truck operators run FieldRoutes or PestPac and bill recurring quarterly. Trigger: image upload from a $50 cellular trail camera mounted inside commercial bait stations at high-value accounts (restaurants, food processors, hospitals). Stack: ESP32 + cellular camera or off-the-shelf Reolink Go, image pipeline through a Claude vision API call with a species-classification prompt fine-tuned on a small labeled set, n8n for the threshold logic and CRM write-back, Twilio for tech dispatch SMS. Role replaced: the monthly route inspection that wastes a tech's morning on stations with zero activity. Pitch sentence: "Your high-value restaurant accounts get a camera in the bait station, and your tech only rolls when activity actually crosses threshold — you stop driving past empty traps and you catch real infestations on day one."

### 4. The two-way SMS cart recovery agent that closes the sale itself — `sollos`, `general`
**Source:** Markopolo AI behavioral cart-recovery agents (https://neuwark.com/blog/best-ai-cart-recovery-tools-shopify-2026) and the n8n smart Shopify abandoned cart agent template (https://n8n.io/workflows/4396-smart-shopify-agent-ai-powered-abandoned-cart-recovery/) — running in production on Shopify SMB stores in 2026, recovering 20–40% of abandoned carts versus 8–12% for static email drips.
**Mechanism:** Shopify "checkout abandoned" webhook fires. Agent reads the cart contents, the customer's session behavior (time on site, products compared, pages bounced from), and any prior purchase history from Klaviyo. It composes a personalized SMS — not a template, an actual question grounded in the specific reason it infers the cart was abandoned (price hesitation, shipping cost surprise, comparison shopping). When the customer replies, it holds a real two-way conversation: answers product questions from a fed-in product knowledge base, offers a bounded discount (e.g., up to 10% only, only once, only if customer asks twice), and sends a one-click checkout link with the discount pre-applied. Hands off to a human only on returns/complaints or anything outside a defined product/discount scope. Replaces the multi-step Klaviyo email flow that gets 25% open rates and 2% conversion.
**Why agentic:** It runs a real conversation with a real customer, with bounded authority to negotiate price within a policy. That's a multi-turn decision agent, not a templated drip. The behavioral signal interpretation (why did this person actually bounce) is the agentic piece.
**SMB translation:** Stephen at Sollos directly, and a productized version for the broader Shopify CPG cohort once it works there. Sollos sells single-cans and 12-packs; AOV bounces around $25–$70 and we've talked about subscription. Trigger: Shopify abandoned checkout webhook with phone number consent. Stack: Shopify webhooks → n8n → Claude API with a product-knowledge-base prompt, Twilio SMS, Shopify Discount Code API for the bounded-authority discount, Klaviyo for the customer history pull. Role replaced/augmented: the static Klaviyo abandoned-checkout flow Sollos almost certainly has running today. Pitch sentence: "When someone bails on checkout, they get a text from us within 10 minutes asking the right question about why they bailed — and the bot can offer up to 10% off to close it without your involvement."
