# 2026-04-17 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
Stripe's "minions" show the real pattern worth stealing: a Slack emoji reaction is the trigger, not a form or a command. That same idea (a one-click label = "you handle this") is the shortest path to an IEC agent for Jenn that drafts per-student parent updates without her writing another email.

Also live today: an anti-dashboard KPI narrative agent for Slack, a standup-collector that chases non-responders, and Avoca-style inbound voice booking for trades.

## Patterns

### 1. Reaction-as-trigger task handoff — `jenn`
**Source:** [How Stripe built "minions"](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-stripe-built-minionsai-coding) — How I AI, Steve Kaliski (Stripe eng), 2026-03-25. Stripe ships ~1,300 PRs/week this way.
**Mechanism:** Engineer adds a specific Slack emoji reaction to a message (bug report, ask, screenshot). That reaction is the trigger. Agent spins up an isolated cloud dev env, reads the thread for context, makes the change, opens a PR with a Slack backlink. Human reviews; no branch cutting, no ticketing, no context switch. Replaces the "I'll pick this up later" promise that never actually happens.
**Why agentic:** The trigger is an ambient, in-flow human signal (one click in the tool you're already in), not a structured form or command. Context is pulled from the surrounding conversation. Action produces an artifact in a different system. There is no dashboard to "check."
**SMB translation:** Jenn (IEC). Trigger: Gmail label `needs-update` applied to any student thread, or a Loom/doc shared in a shared Drive folder. Agent reads (a) the full thread, (b) the student's record in CollegePlannerPro (or CounselMore — verify which she uses), (c) the last parent update sent. Drafts the next parent update as a Gmail draft in her name, with the week's college-list movement, essay status, and open questions, and pings her in Slack/SMS "draft ready for Emma Chen." Stack: Gmail API + Claude API + a tiny MCP shim around her CRM export, plus a Google Apps Script label watcher. Replaces: 30–45 min of her writing each Sunday per active client (so ~15 hrs/month at 20 clients). Pitch: "You already label your inbox. Let the label be the work order. You get a draft; you send or edit. No portal, no dashboard, no 'where did we leave off.'"

### 2. KPI narrative agent (anti-dashboard Friday summary) — `caped`
**Source:** [AI-powered Slack workflows for PMs who hate dashboards](https://blog.logrocket.com/product-management/ai-powered-slack-workflows-for-product-managers) — LogRocket, with a concrete example described at [Cotera](https://cotera.co/articles/slack-workflow-builder-advanced-guide). Agent runs every Friday 4 PM, pulls a Google Sheet, posts to `#ops`.
**Mechanism:** Scheduled trigger. Reads a KPI sheet plus the prior week's post. Computes week-over-week deltas, identifies where change is concentrated, and writes a summary with causal framing — e.g., "Support volume up 23%, highest WoW since January, concentrated in Salesforce connector issues." Posts to Slack. Replaces the weekly report and the "can you put together a deck for Monday" scramble.
**Why agentic:** It doesn't just display the numbers — it explains which bucket the change is in and compares against its own prior post. That framing is synthesis, not a chart.
**SMB translation:** Caped (SAT/ACT prep). Trigger: cron every Monday 7 AM. Reads: Blueprint/TestInnovators score exports + attendance sheet + tutor notes doc. Posts to Debbie's Slack (or WhatsApp via a webhook) one message per active cohort: "Cohort 7 practice-test scores flat this week (avg 1320, +5), but Math -20 driven by three students skipping Saturday session. Sarah up 60. Jake missed again — third time in 4 weeks." Stack: Sheets + Claude API + a Make.com scheduled scenario; ~$30/mo. Replaces: a director pulling numbers and writing a Monday status email. Pitch: "Instead of building you a dashboard that nobody opens, we send the conclusions that would have been on it. One message per cohort, no login."

### 3. Standup-collector with follow-up enforcement — `music-space`
**Source:** [Cotera — Slack Workflow Builder Topped Out at Step 3](https://cotera.co/articles/slack-workflow-builder-advanced-guide). Agent asks at 9 AM, DMs non-responders at 9:45, escalates to manager on a third skip.
**Mechanism:** Scheduled prompt to each person. Reads who replied vs. didn't. Sends a DM nudge at +45 min. Keeps a rolling count per person across the week; if threshold hit, it DMs the manager with the specific pattern ("Jen skipped Mon/Wed/Fri"). Replaces: the "can everyone please actually fill out the form" Slack message that a manager sends twice a week forever.
**Why agentic:** It has state (streak counting), a conditional escalation, and a different action depending on the count. A webhook or a Google Form cannot do that on its own.
**SMB translation:** Justin at The Music Space. Trigger: every Sunday 6 PM, agent DMs each teacher in Opus1 (or via Twilio SMS if they're not in Slack) "quick notes on your 6 students this week?" Reads: the teacher's booked lessons from Opus1 for the week. Follows up at Mon 9 AM if missing; pings Justin with the laggards on Wed. Outputs: each teacher's notes auto-file into the student's Opus1 record so parents see them. Stack: Opus1 API (or CSV export via Make) + Twilio + Claude API. Replaces: Justin personally chasing teachers on Slack. Pitch: "Teachers get one SMS with their student list pre-filled. Parents get notes. You stop being the nag."

### 4. Inbound voice booking agent for trades — `hvac`, `landscaping`, `pest-control`
**Source:** [Owned and Operated #173 — "We Booked 400 Calls a Week"](https://www.ownedandoperated.com/post/owned-and-operated-173-we-booked-400-calls-a-week-how-avoca-ai-is-shaping-home-services) (Avoca). Newer episode [#194 on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/episode/48Q1HXXG25F8mHT4RVVJyl) covers the same pattern.
**Mechanism:** Call router forwards inbound and after-hours calls to a voice agent. Agent reads: recent call history (this is a repeat caller?), tech calendar from ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro, service-area + pricing guardrails. Action: qualifies (is this a real job or a cold call from a marketer), books the slot or deposits a callback task, sends confirmation SMS with address and window. Writes to: CRM as a new job with tags. Replaces: a CSR answering at night and on weekends; also triages the 30% of inbound that are junk.
**Why agentic:** The agent holds a multi-turn conversation, consults external systems live, and can decline or defer. It's not a phone tree.
**SMB translation:** Any of our cold-outreach targets (HVAC, landscaping, pest). Trigger: Twilio/phone-system SIP forward on ring-no-answer or after-hours. Stack: Vapi or Retell for voice + Claude API for dialogue + ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro/Jobber API for calendar; $200–600/mo all-in at SMB volume. Replaces: missed-call voicemail and the "we'll call you back Monday" that kills 40% of leads. Pitch: "You already pay for leads. Stop losing the ones that call after 5 PM." This is how we open a landscaping or HVAC cold conversation — the prospect's pain is measurable (count of missed calls on the monthly phone bill) and the ROI math is arithmetic, not a case study.

### 5. Supply-chain ping agent (autonomous buyer/founder nudge) — `sollos`
**Source:** [Crisp Launches AI Agents for CPG Brands and Retailers](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260211212237/en/Crisp-Launches-AI-Agents-to-Drive-Shareholder-Value-for-CPG-Brands-and-Retailers) — Feb 2026.
**Mechanism:** Agent monitors distributor/retailer inventory and sell-through feeds. Detects low inventory at a specific DC or velocity spike on a SKU. Drafts a concrete replenishment recommendation (unit count, target DC, date) and contacts the retail buyer directly with the ask. Replaces: a category manager manually reviewing the weekly Syndigo/SPINS pull and then emailing the buyer.
**Why agentic:** It reaches out. Enterprise tooling is mostly a dashboard that surfaces the same alert and waits for a human to act. Making the outreach is the whole point.
**SMB translation:** Stephen at Sollos is pre-retail and too small to have distributor telemetry. The applicable mechanism at his scale is the inverse: watch his own ops surfaces for supply risk and ping him specifically. Trigger: daily cron + a Gmail watcher on his co-packer and ingredient supplier threads. Reads: QuickBooks/Shopify order volume (or even a Google Sheet if that's all he has), Gmail threads with yerba supplier, co-packer schedule sheet. Action: if next 30-day forecast volume > on-hand inventory at co-packer, or if a supplier email contains delay language ("pushed," "backordered," "ETA"), the agent drafts an SMS to Stephen with the SKU, the gap, and a suggested next step (which supplier to email, when). Stack: Gmail API + Sheets + Claude API + Twilio. Maybe $40/mo. Replaces: the 2 AM "wait, do we have enough tea for the Whole Foods order" spiral. Pitch: "You don't need an ERP. You need a pager that wakes up before you do, and only for things that actually matter."
