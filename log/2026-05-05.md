# 2026-05-05 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
The month-end close automation agent has compressed an 8-day process to under 3 days, completing journal entries, reconciliations, and variance analysis in minutes while generating audit-ready workpapers. Same pattern works for any professional services firm with recurring financial reporting cycles.

## Patterns

### 1. The month-end close agent that works on calendar triggers
*(tags: `accountants`, `small-law-firms`, `medical-practices`, `consultants`)*

OpenAI's accounting team built an agent that prepares key parts of month-end close, from journal entries to balance sheet reconciliations to variance analysis, completing the work in minutes and generating workpapers with underlying inputs and control totals needed for review. The agent follows internal policies during execution and has memory to improve over time. When month-end approaches, the agent automatically triggers based on calendar dates, pulls general ledger balances, retrieves supporting documentation, performs automated matching, identifies variances, and drafts explanations for reviewer approval ([OpenAI Workspace Agents, May 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-workspace-agents-in-chatgpt/)).

At a small law firm, the same mechanism triggers when the month-end calendar hits. The agent connects to the firm's practice management system and accounting software, automatically categorizes billable time by matter and client, reconciles trust account balances against client obligations, generates monthly partnership distributions, and prepares variance analysis comparing budgeted versus actual expenses by practice area. It knows which journal entries are recurring, which bank reconciliations need manual review, and flags unusual expense patterns for partner attention. Stack is practice management API plus accounting system integration plus Claude for variance analysis plus automated workpaper generation. Around $600-800 per month including platform costs. Replaces the office manager spending 40 hours monthly on close tasks and the CPA consultant billing $200/hour for 8 hours of monthly reconciliation work. Pitch: your books close themselves every month, compliance stays current, and you spend zero time on financial administration.

At a medical practice managing multiple revenue streams, the agent handles insurance claim reconciliations, patient payment processing, provider compensation calculations, and regulatory reporting requirements. It reconciles payments against submitted claims, identifies outstanding receivables, calculates provider productivity metrics, and ensures HIPAA-compliant documentation throughout the close process. Around $400-700 per month for single-provider practices. Replaces the practice administrator juggling financial close tasks and the bookkeeper working extra hours during busy periods.
