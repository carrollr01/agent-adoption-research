# 2026-11-03 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
Five patterns surfaced today. The most interesting is the end-of-day "digest" agent that replaces a day of Linear notifications with one synthesized briefing — a push-based alternative to the standard notification firehose. Also notable: a rule-based policy engine wrapping a voice LLM for regulated clinical calls, a weekly cross-SaaS churn-sniffing agent, a franchise cash reconciliation workflow, and a pre-sales qualification agent cutting the share of deals that need a human by about half.

## Patterns

### 1. End-of-day Linear digest agent
**Source:** [Roboto Studio — Building agents with eve](https://robotostudio.com/blog/building-agents-on-eve) — a small studio rebuilt its internal "catch me up on Linear" tool on Vercel's eve framework (post dated 2026-11-02); forked from Vercel Labs' personal-agent-template.
**Mechanism:** End-of-day cron fires → agent reads all Linear activity in the project workspace since the last digest (new issues, status changes, comments, mentions) → synthesizes a single contextual update ordered by what the user actually needs to act on tomorrow → posts it to Slack/email → replaces the user wading through Linear's native notification feed and inbox digests.
**Why agentic:** The agent decides what matters, in what order, and whether something warrants surfacing at all; Linear's native digest is a dumb concatenation. The output is prose with judgment baked in, not a list of unread items.
**Generalizable core:** Scheduled trigger → agent reads a system of record end-to-end → ranks and narrates what changed → writes one message to a messaging surface the user already reads.
**Deployability:** Vercel eve + Linear channel + Slack or email channel. One-week build. Replaces the task of "logging in Monday and figuring out what happened." Works unchanged for Jira, Asana, GitHub issues, Shopify order activity, QuickBooks AR aging, HubSpot pipeline. Pattern scales down to a solo operator and up to a team of a few hundred.

### 2. Pre-sales qualification agent that routes to checkout or human
**Source:** [How Anthropic's sales team rebuilt inbound with Claude Managed Agents](https://claude.com/resources/articles/how-anthropics-sales-team-rebuilt-inbound-with-claude-managed-agents) — Anthropic's own BD team shipped this on their Contact Sales and Pricing pages; article reports the share of deals needing a human to close fell by about half.
**Mechanism:** Prospect clicks "chat" on pricing or contact page → agent reads existing docs/support articles as its knowledge base + the prospect's typed needs → asks structured follow-up questions about team size, Claude usage, security requirements → recommends a plan and seat count → ends the session by either sending the prospect to checkout, handing them to a BDR with the full transcript and reason for escalation, or giving a quick answer and closing out. Replaces the BDR's job of qualifying every inbound form submission and answering pricing/security questions the docs already covered.
**Why agentic:** It makes a routing decision (self-serve vs. human) per conversation, not a static form fill. It assembles context (docs + conversation state) rather than passing data to a human to read.
**Generalizable core:** Inbound chat on a public page → agent reads product documentation + user's stated situation → classifies the opportunity → takes one of three actions: self-serve conversion, enriched handoff with reason, or answer-and-close.
**Deployability:** Claude Managed Agents (beta) handles hosting, sessions, and tool orchestration; one engineer, few weeks. Weekly prompt iteration via console with staging + versioning. Replaces or augments one BDR seat. Works for any SMB with pricing-page traffic — the mechanism is identical for an MSP, a dental practice group selling implants, a trades business selling installs, or a SaaS firm.

### 3. Weekly cross-SaaS churn-and-expansion scan agent
**Source:** [Zapier — 7 AI agent use cases](https://zapier.com/blog/ai-agent-use-cases/) — Healthie, a health-tech vendor, built this on Zapier Agents; scale not disclosed but Healthie's CS team runs it as a scheduled weekly task.
**Mechanism:** Monday cron → agent queries Salesforce (contract/renewal data), HubSpot (recent marketing/sales touches), Vitally (product usage patterns), and Help Scout (support ticket sentiment and volume) → cross-references these to flag accounts showing early churn signals (usage drop + support complaints + no touches) or expansion signals (usage growth + feature exploration + positive support interactions) → writes a prioritized list to a Slack channel or email with the specific signals per account → replaces the CS manager's job of manually assembling that view across four tools every Monday.
**Why agentic:** The join across four systems is the work; a dashboard would show four separate views and leave the correlation to the human. The agent decides what counts as a signal strong enough to flag.
**Generalizable core:** Schedule trigger → agent reads multiple systems-of-record → correlates signals across them → writes a short, ranked, reason-tagged list to a human's channel of choice.
**Deployability:** Zapier Agents, Make, or n8n all handle this. Few-day build if the four tools have decent APIs. Replaces 2–4 hours of CS manager time per week. The pattern copies to any sector where the "state of the customer" is spread across booking, billing, usage, and support: a solo real-estate agent (MLS + Zillow leads + Follow Up Boss + Gmail), a dental practice (Dentrix + review platform + call logs + payment history), a trades business (ServiceTitan + QuickBooks + Google reviews + text messages).

### 4. Rule-based policy engine wrapping a voice LLM for regulated calls
**Source:** [OpenAI — How Anima helps clinics cut call waits by 75%](https://openai.com/index/anima/) — Anima deploys "Andi," an AI receptionist, across UK primary care clinics; customer story dated 2026-10-23.
**Mechanism:** Patient calls the clinic → GPT-Live answers → a clinically approved rule-based policy engine sits between the model and the conversation, routing it through approved question paths (symptom intake, urgency triage, demographic verification) → GPT-6 extracts structured information from the call and from any inbound clinical documents (sender, recipient, document type, event dates) → writes the triaged record to the clinic's patient system → routes urgent cases to a human, routine ones to self-service booking. Replaces the receptionist's job of answering phones and manually logging symptom information.
**Why agentic:** The LLM is doing the conversation, but the policy engine is the actor — it decides which branch of the clinical script runs, when to escalate, and when to end the call. It is a bounded agent inside a hard-coded guardrail layer, which is the shape most regulated voice deployments actually need.
**Generalizable core:** Inbound real-time channel → LLM runs the conversation inside a rule engine that enforces approved paths → agent extracts structured output → writes to the system of record → escalates on policy triggers.
**Deployability:** GPT-Live or Vapi/Retell for voice; a policy layer written as a state machine or decision table; integration into the vertical SaaS (clinic PMS, dental PMS, legal matter system). Month-build range because the policy work is the hard part, not the model. Replaces or augments phone receptionist. The pattern copies cleanly to any regulated or legally sensitive inbound call: law firm intake, insurance claims first notice of loss, mortgage lead intake, pharmacy refill lines.

### 5. Franchise cash reconciliation agent
**Source:** [OpenAI — Ted Taylor restaurant case study](https://openai.com/index/ted-taylor-restaurant/) — a single franchisee of three co-branded Schlotzsky's/Cinnabon restaurants in New Mexico; customer story dated 2026-10-20.
**Mechanism:** On a daily or weekly schedule → ChatGPT Work agent pulls POS daily sales reports, bank deposit records, and franchisor sales reports → reconciles the three sources to find missing deposits, wrong settlement amounts, or franchisor underreporting → produces a reconciliation summary with discrepancies flagged → writes it to the owner's inbox or a shared drive → replaces the owner's own weekly multi-hour manual spreadsheet reconciliation.
**Why agentic:** The reconciliation is a judgment task — matching POS line items to deposit totals requires fuzzy matching across different grouping windows, not an exact SQL join. The agent iterates until it has a reconciled view, then reports what it couldn't match.
**Generalizable core:** Schedule trigger → agent pulls records from 2–4 independent financial systems → reconciles across different data shapes → writes a discrepancy report to the operator's inbox.
**Deployability:** ChatGPT Work or Claude for Small Business running against POS exports (Toast, Square, Clover), bank statements via Plaid, and franchisor portal exports. Weekend build for a single-restaurant case, week build if the POS integration is messy. Replaces 3–5 hours of owner or bookkeeper time per week. Pattern copies to any SMB where money moves through multiple systems that don't reconcile cleanly: a dental practice (practice management + payment processor + insurance EOBs), a trades business (ServiceTitan + QuickBooks + payment processors), a gym (Mindbody + Stripe + merchant deposits).
