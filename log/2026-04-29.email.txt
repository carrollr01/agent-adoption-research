Avoca closed $125M+ at a $1B valuation on April 27, and the more interesting half of their product is now outbound. Agents wake up unsold estimates older than 30/60/90 days and lapsed customers, run multi-channel SMS-then-call sequences, and book the response straight into ServiceTitan. They cite reactivating a $15K estimate that had been sitting for eight months. The same outbound mechanism drops directly into Justin's music studio (lapsed students who quit during a move or exam season) and Debbie at Capital Educators (discovery-call leads that never enrolled). Stack is HubSpot or Opus1 plus Twilio plus Claude — $80–250 per month at SMB scale. Variation of the 4-17 inbound voice agent pattern with a meaningfully new angle.

Solea's pest-control AI platform ships an "AI Coach" that scores every CSR call against a fixed rubric, writes coaching notes back to the CRM, and DMs the rep their two worst clips. Adjacent confirmation from Nooks AI Coach and Gong's January 2026 AI Call Reviewer update — same mechanism running in B2B sales. For Capital Educators this is a five-item rubric on every parent discovery call (test date, target schools, current score, prior tutoring, budget) plus a Friday roll-up to Debbie. For Jenn it scores parent intake calls. Around $150 per month for a 3-rep team. Replaces the Sunday-evening listen-to-two-calls discipline that doesn't actually happen.

Crunchtime announced Photo Intelligence and AI Actions on April 21 — manager takes a photo, AI grades against standards, agent auto-creates follow-up tasks. The cleanest SMB translation is landscaping: the foreman texts a "job done" photo on his way to the truck, the agent checks edging, mulch coverage, and debris cleanup against the work order in Aspire or LMN, then either triggers the invoice and review-request SMS or creates a punch-list task before he leaves. Stack is Twilio plus Claude with vision plus Aspire/LMN webhook plus QuickBooks. Around $200–400 per month. Replaces the owner driving to every job before billing.
