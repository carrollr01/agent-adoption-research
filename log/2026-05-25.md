# 2026-05-25 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
One qualifying pattern emerged: automated invoice monitoring and payment chasing agents triggered by accounting system events. The pattern represents a shift from reactive billing follow-up to proactive accounts receivable management that works while business owners sleep.

## Patterns

### 1. Invoice Chase Automation Agents
*(tags: `professional-services`, `trades`, `small-construction`, `home-health`, `accountants`)*

[Anthropic's Claude for Small Business](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/anthropic-courts-a-new-kind-of-customer-small-business-owners/) launched with a real-world case study from their head of U.S. SMB, Lina Ochman, who built an automated invoice workflow for her mother's business over Christmas. The system monitors QuickBooks invoice creation events as triggers, automatically sends payment reminders via email on predetermined schedules, tracks payment status, and escalates to text or phone follow-ups for overdue accounts. The output replaces the manual task of remembering which invoices are outstanding and when to follow up.

For accounting practices, the same pattern triggers when client invoices hit 30-day overdue status in practice management software like QuickBooks or Sage, automatically generating personalized payment reminders that reference specific services performed, sending escalating communications through email and text via Twilio integration, and creating follow-up tasks in Google Workspace for partners to make personal calls only after automated attempts. The pitch: "Your AI handles the awkward payment conversations until it needs you." For construction companies, agents could trigger from project milestone completion in job management software, automatically sending progress invoices with completion photos attached, monitoring payment schedules against project timelines, and alerting project managers when payment delays might impact cash flow for materials or subcontractors. Home health agencies could deploy similar automation triggered by aide visit completions in scheduling systems, generating family invoices with visit summaries and care notes, tracking insurance claim statuses, and managing the complex billing cycles that span private pay, insurance, and Medicaid reimbursements.
