Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-10-09 — 4 patterns | Puzzle's continuous close replaces the monthly batch

Repo: https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/determined-meitner-poe2za/log/2026-10-09.md

Four concrete patterns today. The one worth sitting with is Puzzle AI Close: it doesn't speed up the monthly accounting close, it dissolves it. Agents reconcile transactions continuously as they arrive from Stripe, Mercury, Ramp, Brex, Gusto, draft accruals and journal entries throughout the month, and queue everything for human sign-off at the usual period cadence. Early adopters report 15-20 day closes dropping to 3-5 days with 98% of transactions auto-categorized. The generalizable shape — fire the agent on each new record, hold outputs in a drafts queue, let humans review at the usual cadence instead of reconstructing the batch at month-end — transplants to any periodic review process. Legal matter billing, construction job-cost close, insurance claims closeouts, payroll.

Carvana's Claude Tag deployment is the clearest "alert-channel-to-pull-request" case to date. A production monitoring alert hits Slack, the agent reads logs, repo, runbooks and a baseline from the hour before the last release, decides which team owns it, posts a root-cause write-up in that team's format, and opens a draft PR with a proposed fix. The nice twist is the hourly cron variant: the same agent runs on a schedule to catch failing pipelines and stale feature flags without an alert firing. Alerts in one channel dropped 56%, answers came back 65% faster on another team. Replaces first-pass on-call triage, not merge authority.

Claire Vo's Merge Mommy — built in a single Codex session on Vercel Eve — is a "scored-triage" pattern worth stealing. After CI passes, the agent scores each PR across six dimensions (change size, blast radius, reversibility, data/security impact, operational impact, test/CI completeness), auto-approves anything below 24 points, pings the author's lead in Slack with its reasoning for anything above 64. The output is a decision about where human attention goes, not a replacement for it. The escalation message carries the reasoning so the human review starts hot. This mechanism moves cleanly to any inbound queue where ~70% is routine: insurance claim intake, lending pre-screens, support ticket severity, grant first-pass review.

Supermetrics for Claude is the clearest MCP-mediated SaaS-operator deployment. A marketer types a question or a command in Claude; Claude calls the Supermetrics MCP; Supermetrics brokers the already-authenticated connection to Google Ads, Meta, Microsoft, TikTok; writes come back live but every new campaign starts paused and every change is undoable in one click. Fourteen tools in the surface (7 read, 5 write). Reports a 10-hour agency report now at 20 minutes. The generalizable primitive is the paused-by-default write: the agent takes the action but gates deployment behind a human flip. The shape fits any vertical where a data vendor is already sitting between operators and raw APIs (PMS, EHR, veterinary PIMS, legal practice management), provided the vendor publishes an MCP server with scoped writes.

Noted and not logged: Sentry's Seer (production error to merge-ready PR in ~6 min via Claude Managed Agents, 600k PRs/mo reviewed) is adjacent enough to Carvana to not double-log. Golden Nugget's PolyAI deployment (34% of reservation calls fully handled, 100% answered) overlaps with the voice-agent entry already in the log. OpenAI's Presence launch (Jul 2026) is a managed-agent platform with embedded FDEs; interesting shape but no public customer mechanism yet.
