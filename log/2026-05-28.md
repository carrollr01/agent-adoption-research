## Patterns

### 1. Project Milestone Invoicing Automation
*(tags: `professional-services`, `small-construction`, `trades`, `design-agencies`, `consultants`)*

[Anthropic's Claude for Small Business](https://quasa.io/media/anthropic-launches-claude-for-small-business-ai-agents-that-work-inside-the-tools-you-already-use) showcases a design agency workflow where agents monitor project milestone completion in Asana as the trigger, automatically generate professional invoices in QuickBooks using project details, create branded templates through Canva integration, and send formatted invoices via email. The system replaces the manual task of tracking project progress, calculating billing amounts, and creating client communications for each milestone reached.

For small construction companies, the same trigger pattern works when project phases complete in job management software like BuilderTREND or CoConstruct, with agents pulling completion photos and progress notes to generate milestone invoices with visual proof of work, sending them automatically to clients with payment terms and next-phase timelines. The stack integrates job management systems, QuickBooks for invoicing, Canva for visual formatting, and email delivery through Google Workspace or Outlook, replacing the project manager's weekly task of manually tracking completions and creating milestone billing. The pitch: "Your jobs invoice themselves when phases complete, with photos attached and payment tracking automated." For consulting firms, agents could trigger from deliverable approvals in project management tools, automatically generating invoices with work summaries, attaching final deliverables, and scheduling follow-up conversations, transforming the billing process from a monthly administrative burden into an automatic completion reward for successful project phases.
