# 2026-05-08 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
The medical billing agent handles the complete revenue cycle from clinical notes to insurance payment, automating coding, error-checking, and denial management while keeping physicians in control of approvals. Same pattern works for any professional services firm with complex regulatory documentation requirements.

## Patterns

### 1. The medical billing agent that codes while you document
*(tags: `medical-practices`, `dental-and-vet-clinics`, `accountants`, `small-law-firms`)*

Taiga automates medical billing end-to-end where doctors upload visit data and AI figures out the right billing codes, checks claims for errors, and submits them to insurance while handling any denials. The agent processes clinical notes to generate ICD-10 and CPT codes, reviews each claim against payer rules before submission, automatically appeals denied claims, and integrates directly with Epic, Athena, eClinicalWorks, and other major EHRs without workflow changes. Unlike simple coding tools, this handles the complete revenue cycle with physician approval checkpoints but no manual administrative work ([Taiga, Y Combinator W26](https://usetaiga.com/)).

At a small law firm managing employment cases, the same mechanism triggers when attorneys complete case documentation in their practice management system. The agent reads case notes and client communications, identifies billable activities and categorizes them by appropriate billing codes for different clients, cross-references work against retainer agreements and billing rate structures, generates timesheets with detailed descriptions, and flags potential ethical billing issues or conflicts before submission to clients. It knows which activities qualify for different billing rates, what expenses are reimbursable under each client agreement, and when to escalate unusual billing situations to partner review. Stack is practice management system integration plus legal billing database plus Claude for activity classification plus automated invoicing systems. Around $800-1200 per month for multi-partner firms. Replaces the billing coordinator spending 20 hours weekly categorizing time entries and the office manager reconciling client accounts. Pitch: your billing writes itself from case work, compliance stays current, and you never lose billable time to administrative delays.

At a dental practice with multiple providers, the agent handles procedure coding, insurance pre-authorization requests, and patient billing workflows while ensuring compliance with dental insurance requirements and fee schedules. It processes treatment notes to generate proper dental procedure codes, submits pre-authorization requests with supporting documentation, tracks approval status and manages appeals, and coordinates with patient payment plans. Around $600-900 per month for multi-provider practices. Replaces the office manager handling insurance paperwork and the billing specialist managing patient accounts.
