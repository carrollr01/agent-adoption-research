Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-06-28 — 2 patterns | Verifier sub-agents gating artifacts before humans see them

https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/determined-meitner-07raad/log/2026-06-28.md

Two patterns this run, both built around a verifier or persona sub-agent that gates an artifact before any human sees it.

Mozilla's Firefox security pipeline pairs a bug-hunter agent with an adversarial verifier sub-agent. The hunter runs continuously over Firefox source with bash, browser, and fuzz tooling; the verifier re-runs each finding and rejects "wonky" ones (e.g., the hunter introducing a vulnerability just to satisfy the goal); verified findings emit in a forced schema and auto-file directly into Bugzilla. 271 fixed bugs attributed to the pipeline, 180 high-severity. The generalizable core: continuous data sweep → hunter with execution tools → adversarial verifier sub-agent → schema-locked output auto-files into the team's existing ticketing system. Applies to PR review queues, contract backlogs, claims-fraud triage, ops incident pre-filtering. Source: How I AI episode June 21, 2026 with Brian Grinstead.

Quantium (1,200-person data analytics consulting firm) runs proposal drafts past "digital twins" of senior leaders before the real executive review. A drafter agent pulls from the firm's prior-wins corpus and writes the bid response; then a second layer of agents — each conditioned on a specific senior leader's historical reviews and feedback — critiques the draft in that person's voice and judgment style. The human executive only enters at final sign-off. Proposal cycle time dropped from weeks to hours. The generalizable core: inbound artifact → drafter pulls from prior-wins corpus → persona-conditioned reviewer agent simulates the gate review → human only enters at final sign-off. Applies anywhere a single person's review is the bottleneck (legal memos, design crits, deal memos, grant applications, arch review docs). Hard prerequisite: meaningful written feedback history from the person being modeled. Source: Anthropic customer story published June 22, 2026.
