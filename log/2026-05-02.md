# 2026-05-02 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
Corvera (YC W26) just launched an agent that reads retailer purchase orders out of a CPG brand's inbox, matches the line items against the brand's SKU catalog, and walks the order through fulfillment-to-invoice with human-in-the-loop approvals. They went $0 → $33k MRR in four weeks across twelve brands. This is the agent we should be building for Sollos. Different direction from the 4/17 Crisp supply pager — that one watched distributor feeds for trouble; this one does the order-entry job a person used to do.

## Patterns

### 1. The retailer PO that books itself — `sollos`

**Source:** [Corvera Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/PES-corvera-ai-agents-for-cpg-brands), [company site](https://www.corvera.ai/) — Corvera (YC W26), launched mid-March 2026, $33k MRR in 4 weeks across 12 CPG brands, 130% week-over-week growth.

**Mechanism:** A retailer emails a PO to the brand's orders inbox, usually as a PDF, sometimes in the email body, occasionally via an EDI portal. Corvera reads the message, parses the PDF, normalizes the line items against the brand's master SKU catalog (handles abbreviations, retailer-specific item codes, pack-size variations), checks live inventory across the brand's warehouses, and writes the order into the brand's ERP or 3PL system. It generates the pick ticket, the ASN, and the invoice. A person in Slack gets pinged only on the exceptions: short ship, SKU not in catalog, credit hold, retailer chargeback risk. Separately the agent reads sales history continuously and forecasts demand, flagging projected stockouts before they happen and recommending POs to the supplier.

**Why agentic:** This is not a dashboard or an alert. The agent does the work — same job that a brand's ops coordinator does at 9 PM matching the Whole Foods PDF against the Cin7 catalog and typing the order. The brand only sees the items the agent couldn't resolve. And it carries context across orders, so the second time the same retailer sends "OG-12CT" the agent already knows it's the 12-pack of Original.

**SMB translation:**
- **Vertical:** Sollos directly. Also any pre-launch or sub-$10M CPG brand selling into specialty grocery, Faire, Whole Foods, Erewhon, regional distributors. The 5–50 person brand whose founder is doing PO entry at 11 PM.
- **Trigger:** new email landing in `orders@<brand>.com` from a known retailer or distributor domain. Backup trigger: row added to a shared Google Sheet that the 3PL drops orders into.
- **Stack:** Gmail watcher (Apps Script or Make) plus Claude with PDF parsing plus an SKU master in Airtable or a Google Sheet plus write-back to Shopify, QuickBooks, or whatever 3PL portal the brand uses. Slack DMs for the exception queue. Probably $300–600 a month all-in at Sollos's volume.
- **Role replaced:** Stephen at 11 PM on Tuesday. Or the part-time ops contractor he's about to hire and doesn't want to.
- **Pitch sentence:** "Retailer POs land in your inbox, the order is built and routed before you read the email, and you only touch the ones the agent flags."

This is the direct complement to the 4/17 Crisp pager, not a duplicate. That one was inbound supply telemetry — watch the supplier email thread for "delayed" and warn the chef. This one is inbound demand execution — read the customer PO and run it. A real Sollos build would probably want both, but the PO-handler is the bigger hour-saver.
