# 2026-05-12 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
The broker negotiation agent automates trucking dispatch's most tedious work—calling brokers to negotiate rates and confirm details—using natural conversation to query market rates and handle deals end-to-end. This pattern works for any business needing human-to-human negotiation at scale.

## Patterns

### 1. The broker negotiation agent that calls while you focus elsewhere
*(tags: `small-construction`, `real-estate`, `consultants`, `insurance`)*

Numeo's Spot Finder Pro uses "Agentic AI" voice agents that hold natural conversations with freight brokers, query real-time market rates from DAT and other boards, negotiate based on the carrier's profitability goals, and only involve the dispatcher when a deal is ready to sign. The agent triggers when new loads appear on freight boards, reads current market data and carrier parameters, makes voice calls to brokers using natural conversation, negotiates rates autonomously following preset profitability rules, and presents completed deals for final approval. This replaces the dispatcher spending hours daily calling brokers to check availability and negotiate prices on loads that may not even be viable ([Numeo AI, 2026](https://numeo.ai/)).

At a small construction company bidding commercial projects, the same mechanism triggers when new project opportunities appear on construction bidding platforms like BuildingConnected or PlanHub. The agent reads project specifications and scope documents, identifies relevant subcontractors and suppliers from the company's vendor database, makes voice calls to negotiate pricing and availability for key components like concrete, steel, and specialized labor, compares quotes against project budgets and margins, and presents pre-negotiated pricing packages to the project manager for final proposal assembly. It knows which vendors offer volume discounts, have reliable delivery schedules, and can meet specific project timelines. Stack is bidding platform APIs plus vendor contact database plus Claude for price analysis plus Twilio for voice calls. Around $800-1200 per month for multi-project contractors. Replaces the estimator spending 15 hours weekly calling vendors for pricing that may have changed since the last project. Pitch: your bids include current market pricing negotiated in real-time, you never lose opportunities waiting for vendor callbacks, and profit margins stay protected through automated negotiation rules.

At a real estate investment firm sourcing wholesale deals, the agent monitors distressed property listings, contacts property owners and wholesalers directly via phone, negotiates initial purchase terms based on comparable sales data, and queues qualified deals for investor review. Around $600-900 per month for multi-property investors. Replaces the acquisition manager cold-calling leads and the analyst researching comps for every potential deal.

### 2. The complete business operations agent that builds itself
*(tags: `small-construction`, `consultants`, `small-law-firms`, `accountants`)*

YC W26 company Modern helps IT, HR, and Finance teams automatically resolve 80%+ of high-volume requests by letting teams define workflows in plain language, set approval rules and policies, and handling everything else including password resets, access provisioning, expense approvals, onboarding checklists, and more. The agent triggers on incoming requests through email, Slack, or internal portals, reads request details and matches them against pre-defined workflow templates, executes multi-step processes including system integrations and approval routing, and provides status updates to requesters throughout the process. Unlike simple ticketing systems, this handles the complete workflow from request to resolution with human oversight only when exceptions occur ([Modern, YC W26](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/modern)).

At a small construction company managing project workflows, the same mechanism triggers when project requests arrive through customer portals, email, or phone calls. The agent reads project specifications and customer requirements, matches them against service offerings and capacity schedules, generates work orders with material lists and crew assignments, routes permits and regulatory approvals through appropriate channels, and tracks project status from initiation to completion. It knows which permits are required for different project types, which crews have availability for specific work, and when to escalate scheduling conflicts to project managers. Stack is project management system integration plus permitting API access plus crew scheduling software plus Claude for workflow orchestration. Around $1000-1500 per month for multi-crew contractors. Replaces the office manager coordinating between sales, scheduling, and crews and the project coordinator tracking permits and approvals. Pitch: projects execute themselves from initial inquiry to final inspection, regulatory compliance stays current, and you never lose time to coordination overhead between office and field operations.

At a small law firm handling estate planning, the agent manages the complete client workflow from initial consultation through document execution. It processes client intake forms, generates document checklists based on estate complexity, coordinates document preparation with paralegals, manages client review cycles, and schedules signing appointments with notaries. Around $800-1200 per month for multi-attorney practices. Replaces the paralegal managing document workflows and the office manager coordinating client communications.
