# 2026-09-30 — Agent Pattern Scan

## TL;DR
Long gap since the last log — the standout is Erewhon's 39-step Zap that autonomously answers ~70% of customer service tickets by routing on membership status and drafting from an institutional-knowledge vector store. The other three: a law-firm pre-check agent that turns drafters into their own first-pass reviewer, a field-sales agent that fires the CRM-update pipeline on connection-restore rather than on meeting-end, and a real-estate brokerage where every new lead interaction re-scores an 11,000-record database and re-writes each rep's morning brief.

## Patterns

### 1. Membership-aware CS ticket agent with vector-stored institutional knowledge
**Source:** [Zapier customer story — Erewhon (via casestudies.com)](https://www.casestudies.com/company/zapier/case-study/erewhon-automates-70-of-customer-service-tickets-with-zapier) — Erewhon, 10-location luxury grocery chain in LA. Built by Chris Morrison (business analyst / AI lead), not an engineer.
**Mechanism:** New ticket lands in Help Scout → a 39-step Zap checks the sender's membership status via BigQuery lookup → members route to a dedicated ChatGPT persona with a warmer tone; non-members route to the general responder → both pull from a vector store of Erewhon's institutional knowledge (membership policies, product info, store SOPs) to draft the reply → for high-spend members the Zap also surfaces recent purchase history into a Slack channel store managers watch → drafted reply is posted back into Help Scout for send. ~70% send without human modification. Replaces the CS rep's read-classify-lookup-draft loop and the store manager's "who is this angry customer" triage. Reported outcomes: 1,500 labor hours/year saved, ~$40K equivalent, 1M tasks/year across 89 active Zaps.
**Why agentic:** The Zap is not a linear pipeline — the membership check bifurcates persona and tone, the vector store is queried dynamically per ticket, and the "surface purchase history to a human" branch fires only for members over a spend threshold. Different tickets take different paths through the same automation without a human deciding which.
**Generalizable core:** Inbound support ticket → agent enriches with customer's tier/history from the system of record → routes to a tier-appropriate persona → drafts reply grounded in a vector-stored policy corpus → auto-sends or escalates with context, per confidence.
**Deployability:** Zapier + Help Scout + BigQuery + a vector store (Pinecone/Weaviate/pgvector) + OpenAI or Claude — weekend build for a bare version, week build for the tier-routing and knowledge-store curation. Replaces the L1 support rep almost entirely for a bounded product/policy surface. Requires disciplined knowledge-base upkeep; the ceiling on send-without-edit rate is set by how clean and current the vector store is, not by the model.

### 2. Drafter-side legal pre-check agent
**Source:** [Freshfields + Anthropic multi-year collaboration](https://legaltechnology.com/freshfields-and-anthropic-enter-multi-year-collaboration-agreement/) plus [Anthropic's Claude for Legal launch](https://claude.com/de/blog/deploying-claude-across-the-legal-industry) — 5,700-employee global law firm; open-source suite of 12 practice-area plugins, 80+ specialized agents, ~20 MCP connectors (iManage, NetDocuments, Lexis, Thomson Reuters, Ironclad, DocuSign, Relativity, Word/Outlook).
**Mechanism:** Business partner (a non-lawyer inside the firm's client) drafts a contract or memo in Word → clicks "Analyze Content" → the agent reads the draft plus the relevant playbook via iManage/NetDocuments MCP connectors and Lexis/TR precedent → returns a prioritized issue list (highest-risk items flagged, with citation) and proposed redlines inline → the drafter self-corrects before submitting → the lawyer receives a much cleaner draft with the pre-check log attached and spends their time on the residual judgment calls. Cuts typical review times from days to hours per Anthropic's internal legal team using the same pattern.
**Why agentic:** The check isn't a static lint — the agent picks which playbook applies from the document type, reaches across multiple connected systems for precedent, and its output feeds a human loop that changes the input on the next iteration.
**Generalizable core:** Document authored in system of record → agent reads it against a policy/playbook corpus + comparable precedents → returns prioritized issues and inline suggested edits → author self-corrects → expert reviewer receives the pre-checked version plus the audit trail. Applies far beyond law: vendor contracts, HR policy exceptions, engineering RFCs, insurance underwriting, grant proposals.
**Deployability:** Claude for Legal is open-source (Apache 2.0) so the connector patterns are readable. Week-to-month build outside law because the playbook curation is the actual work. Replaces the first-pass associate/paralegal (in law) or the senior-reviewer bottleneck (elsewhere). Scale caveat: real value only shows when the corpus of precedents and the playbooks are already good enough that a first-pass reviewer would agree with the agent 80%+ of the time.

### 3. Connection-restore-triggered field-sales admin agent
**Source:** [Showpad Field Seller Agent — July 2026 release notes](https://therev.showpad.com/product-updates/showpad-july-2026-field-seller-agent-496) — reps at manufacturers, med-device firms, and industrial distributors who spend the day in customer sites and vehicles.
**Mechanism:** Rep dictates or types a note on iOS at the site, often with no signal → device transcribes locally and saves every 5 seconds → when the phone reconnects, the *reconnect* is the trigger, not a manual "send" → agent pulls deal context from CRM (Salesforce, Dynamics, SAP Sales Cloud, or HubSpot), assembles a structured meeting summary, drafts the follow-up email, recommends collateral from the content library, and posts a CRM update on the opportunity → rep sees a card summarizing what was done on their next login and can edit or approve the follow-up email before it sends.
**Why agentic:** The trigger is an environmental state change (connectivity), not a user action. The agent chooses which content to recommend and which deal fields to update by reading, not by rule. Sync-and-summarize happens across four different CRMs from the same input.
**Generalizable core:** Deferred/offline input capture → environmental trigger (connectivity, upload, workday-end) fires the agent → agent assembles context from the system of record and enriches the raw capture → writes back into the SoR and drafts human-facing artifacts → operator reviews at their next natural touchpoint. Applies to home-services techs finishing a job, field auditors, medical field reps, delivery drivers writing incident notes.
**Deployability:** Native mobile piece is the hard part (offline whisper + local storage). For a bespoke build: Expo/React Native or Flutter + on-device Whisper + Claude/GPT for summary + CRM SDK. Weekend build for a single-CRM prototype; month for cross-CRM and content recs. Replaces the 60% of a rep's day Salesforce's 2026 State of Sales pins on non-selling admin. Scale caveat: you need a content library and a CRM schema stable enough for an agent to write into without breaking reports.

### 4. Per-rep morning brief agent scored off a central lead database
**Source:** [Zapier + Rush Home ("Russ" agent)](https://www.createwith.com/tool/zapier/updates/zapier-mcp-helps-real-estate-broker-build-custom-ai-agent-to-score-11000-leads) — Rush Home, 8-agent residential brokerage; built by the team leader, not an engineer.
**Mechanism:** Any new interaction with any of 11,000 contacts (inbound call, email, form fill, SMS reply, showing attended) → the interaction event flows into Zapier → Zapier MCP hands Claude the lead record plus the new interaction plus the CRM history → Claude runs a custom scoring algorithm (source, buyer/seller, geo, price band, response timing, call duration) → recalculates the lead's score and writes it back → at 6am each weekday, a scheduled run assembles each of the 8 agents' top-ranked leads for the day, drafts a per-lead follow-up or intro tactic drawn from CRM notes, and sends the brief to each rep's inbox from Russ's own email address. Agents can reply back to Russ to update statuses.
**Why agentic:** Not a report generator — the scoring runs continuously on event, the follow-up tactics are drafted per-lead from unstructured notes, and Russ has its own email address that reps interact with, which means the "agent" is a durable identity the team collaborates with, not a scheduled job. Variation of 2026-05-23 entry (Morning Real Estate Scouting) — new angle: the trigger is any new interaction with an existing lead (continuous re-scoring), not a scan of external listings, and the daily output is per-rep and drafted from CRM notes rather than a shared listing summary.
**Deployability:** Zapier + Zapier MCP + Claude + whatever CRM the brokerage uses (BoomTown, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE). Week build for a no-code version, month if you want per-rep tone tuning. Replaces the sales manager's morning huddle prep and the reps' own decision about who to call first. Scale caveat: works because the scoring weights are tuned to one broker's book — copy-pasting the algorithm without recalibration would be worse than the reps' gut.
