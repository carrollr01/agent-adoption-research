Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-07-10 — 4 patterns | Claim posting agent replaces the dental back office

Full log: https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/determined-meitner-395pj7/log/2026-07-10.md

Four concrete patterns today.

1) Payer remittance to practice management system posting (Lassie). Lassie closed a $35M Series A led by a16z on July 8 and is now deployed in 700+ medical and dental practices, offsetting an estimated 250,000 hours of labor per year. Trigger is an ERA (835 file) from the clearinghouse or a scanned EOB PDF. The agent parses line items, three-way matches against submitted claims, fee schedules, and the day's bank deposit, and posts payments directly into Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental. 70 to 90 percent of line items post automatically. Exceptions get flagged with reasoning. Replaces the insurance coordinator's 80 to 100 hours per month of manual EOB posting. Generalizable core: structured or scanned payment document arrives, agent parses and cross-references against internal record plus bank truth, writes reconciled entries to system of record, flags exceptions. Copies to any AR reconciliation where the payment side arrives in a structured file.

2) Ambient AI-search citation-drift agent that files marketing project tickets (Profound Aim, launched July 2). Continuously samples ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity answers on a brand and its competitors. When it detects a citation drop or sentiment shift, it diagnoses the likely cause, drafts a memo, creates a scoped project with tasks, and routes it to specialist execution agents. Human approves before dispatch. Replaces the analyst who used to eyeball a share-of-voice dashboard every Monday and translate it into a brief. Generalizable core: continuously sample an external stream that reflects standing, detect anomalies against baseline, diagnose the change, draft a scoped work package with rationale, route through approval, dispatch. Broadly reusable for any brand-monitoring problem. Week build for a scaled-down version on Claude API plus a cron plus Linear or Asana.

3) Supervised agent fleet for product data operations (Akeneo Agentic Ziggy, Summer release announced July 8). A supervisor UI over specialized agents doing data modeling, schema mapping, enrichment, and quality checks in the PIM. Trigger is new SKU, marketplace schema change, or scheduled QC. Each agent proposes work in its stage and hands off; a product data manager approves in batch. The pattern skeleton — break a back-office ops function into pipeline stages, assign one agent per stage, human approves in batch — copies to HR onboarding, law firm matter intake, clinic chart abstraction, insurance broker claim intake. Week build for a small-team version on Claude API plus a queue plus a simple approval UI.

4) Dispatch-board-triggered outbound voice recovery agent (Avoca at Sila Services). Sila runs 40+ HVAC/plumbing/electrical brands with 1,200 technicians. Between January and October 2025 Avoca handled ~90% of inbound calls and made 80,000+ outbound calls autonomously. Trigger is a state condition inside the field service management system: open capacity tomorrow, stale lead past threshold, membership due, estimate never converted. Agent reads the customer's history, dials, has a scoped conversation, writes the booked appointment back into ServiceTitan or similar. New angle vs. the May 24 PollyReach entry: the trigger is dispatch-board state, not "give an agent a phone number." Same skeleton copies to dental recall, gym membership recovery, auto-service maintenance reminders, B2B SDR reactivation. Voice stack (Retell/Vapi + Twilio + FSM API) is boring now; the trigger logic is the interesting part. Weekend build for a single trigger, week build for CRM writeback.
