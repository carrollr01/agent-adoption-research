Agent Pattern Scan — 2026-11-18 — 5 patterns | Three-stage ops triage loop lands a scoped-fix PR

https://github.com/carrollr01/agent-adoption-research/blob/claude/determined-meitner-tr7wxl/log/2026-11-18.md

Five patterns today, all from vendor-published customer stories where the mechanism is written in enough detail to borrow. The highest-signal entry is LaunchDarkly's three-stage ops triage loop: a Datadog or Spinnaker alert hits a triage agent that reads monitors, logs, and deploy state and files a Jira plan; a validator agent approves, rewrites, or rejects that plan; an implementation agent then opens a scoped-fix PR in GitHub with the on-call engineer tagged. The validator agent is the trust mechanism — it's the agent-in-the-middle that lets the other two run without human intervention.

Zapier yielded three distinct customer deployments. Healthie runs a Monday-morning scan across Salesforce, HubSpot, Vitally, and Help Scout and posts a ranked churn-and-expansion digest into Slack — scheduled trigger, cross-system reasoning, actionable channel output. NisonCo chains three narrow agents off a call transcript: one extracts action items, one logs the prospect in Relatable and fires a Slack alert, one drafts a follow-up email into Gmail drafts for human review. Erewhon routes inbound Help Scout tickets by first looking up membership status and then dispatching to one of two agents with different tones, scopes, and knowledge bases — routing-before-reasoning rather than letting the model figure out tier itself.

The fifth pattern is from Anthropic's n8n case study: an AI Workflow Builder where Claude assembles n8n automations by making discrete tool calls one at a time (add_node, connect_nodes, configure_node) rather than generating full JSON. Natural-language spec in, inspectable workflow out, roughly three minutes vs 10–30 minutes of manual drag-and-drop. The reusable idea is the step-wise tool-call pattern for any composable structured artifact.
